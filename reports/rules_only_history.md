## Reading

The pass mark in the rulebook: ahead of US shares after Sharesies' costs in the hold-out years, no single year giving more than half the gain, and a worst fall no more than 1.5 times the market's. The ten-share version adds the noise of holding only ten names, which these wide portfolios don't show, so a thin margin after costs is a fail.
