# Deal database: base rates

347 rows in all (46 NZX, 301 ASX); 9 majority-holder buy-outs left out of the rates. 320 resolved deals and 13 pending.

The Australian 2015 to 2024 rows are still incomplete after one follow-up pass (deals/au_hist/followup): some deals could not be reached on free sources, and 113 rows have fewer than five of the six key fields. Treat the rates as provisional.
