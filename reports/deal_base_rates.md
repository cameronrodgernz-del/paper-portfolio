# Deal database: base rates

314 rows in all (46 NZX, 268 ASX); 9 majority-holder buy-outs left out of the rates. 274 resolved deals and 13 pending.

The Australian 2015 to 2024 rows are incomplete: the research hit shared web limits, so some deals are missing and 137 rows have fewer than five of the six key fields. Treat the rates as provisional until the follow-up pass.
