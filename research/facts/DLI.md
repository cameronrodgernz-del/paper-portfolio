# Delta Lithium (ASX:DLI): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 7 points, #5 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? Yes, no business to value yet. Delta has no production and no mining or processing study of any kind. It holds two lithium projects in Western Australia with mineral resources but no ore reserves: Mt Ida, 14.8Mt at 1.21% Li2O, 170ppm Ta2O5 and 0.42% Rb2O, and Yinnetharra, 21.9Mt at 1.0% Li2O and 75ppm Ta2O5 (March 2025 estimate) [1][2]. The first study, a scoping study on Mt Ida, only "commenced" in May 2026, with "results targeted to be released this calendar year" [2]; the September 2026 quarter again reports it as started, not finished [1]. There is no study, so no commodity price, cost or mine life on which a value could rest. The rest of its assets are cash and shares in other listed companies (below).
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. The auditor's opinion on the June 2026 accounts is unmodified [2]. A trading halt on 2 April 2026 lifted the same day, after Delta told the market not to rely on a A$93 billion "in-ground" rubidium value quoted in a newspaper article, which the JORC Code doesn't allow [3].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$35,679 a day (lowest day A$4,550), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. Substantial holders at 11 September 2026: ACN 657 042 218 Pty Ltd 23.14%, Hancock Prospecting 12.07%, Idemitsu Mt Ida 11.36%, Waratah Capital Advisors 10.57% (all from notices filed between November 2023 and January 2024) [2].

The sheet ends here on the "no business to value yet" kill.

What Delta holds, for the record (facts only, no valuation):
- Cash A$48.8m at 30 September 2026 (A$49.6m at 30 June) [1][2]. Exploration and evaluation spending was A$1.5m in the September quarter [1].
- 156,310,994 shares in Ballard Mining (ASX:BM1), the Mt Ida gold business demerged and floated in July 2025: 29.89% of Ballard after Ballard's raising of 16 October 2026 (34.38% before) [4]. The shares are under ASX escrow for 24 months from Ballard's admission in July 2025, so until about July 2027 [5]. At Ballard's close of A$0.585 on 30 October 2026 the holding was worth A$91.4m (Norgate prices file). Delta carries it at A$54.7m under equity accounting [2].
- 5m shares in MEX and 16.1m in JAV [1], worth about A$1.6m at their 30 October closes.
- Cash plus those listed holdings at market comes to about A$141.8m, or A$0.197 a share on 719,762,857 shares [2]. The managing director gave "around AUD$145M" for cash and investments in the September quarterly [1].
- The ranking's "8.3 times earnings" is an accounting gain. FY26 profit of A$13.2m includes a A$16.8m gain on the deemed disposal of part of the Ballard investment and a A$1.7m gain on disposing of the Ballard subsidiary; without them FY26 was a loss of about A$5.3m [2].

## 7. Grade
C, on a named kill fact: no business to value yet. Sections 2 to 6 were not written, as the template requires once a kill check answer is yes.
