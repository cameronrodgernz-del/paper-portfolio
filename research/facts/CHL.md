# Camplify Holdings (ASX:CHL): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 4 points, #54 in the ASX table of reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. An online marketplace that rents out privately owned caravans and campervans (Camplify in Australia, New Zealand, the UK and Spain; PaulCamper in Germany, Austria and the Netherlands), with its own member-owned damage protection scheme (MyWay Mutual). FY26 revenue A$39.2m [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? Yes, going-concern doubt. The auditor, PKF (NS) Audit & Assurance, gave an unmodified opinion on the FY26 accounts with a paragraph headed "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern": it draws attention to note 1, which reports a net loss of A$810,956, operating cash outflows of A$1,248,914 and current liabilities exceeding current assets by A$6,447,230 at 30 June 2026, and says these conditions "indicate that a material uncertainty exists that may cast significant doubt on the Company's ability to continue as a going concern" [1]. The Appendix 4E says the same [3]. The 1H26 review report carried the same paragraph [4].
- Too thin to trade? No, but close. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$12,572 a day (lowest A$161; nine sessions under A$10,000), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No holder is over 50% and no bid or review is announced. But a strategic holder is building a stake: JB Group Tech Pty Ltd (the JB caravan maker's group, whose nominee Li Ping Xue joined the board in December 2025) took 12.71% in an October 2025 placement at A$0.3072, then went to 19.24% (14 December 2025), 20.43% (20 May 2026) and 23.43% (19,264,482 shares, 24 June 2026, after buying 2,466,762 shares for A$487,186, about A$0.20) [5][6][7].

The going-concern paragraph is a kill fact, so the sheet ends here.

For the record and the watch list:
- JB Group's buying took it through 20%, the level above which the Corporations Act allows further buying only by a bid or one of the exceptions, such as 3% every six months. Its June notice does not say which exception it relied on. The annual report lists JB Group Tech at 17,264,482 shares (21.00%) at 30 September 2026, which doesn't match the 19,264,482 in the June notice and no later notice; the June notice's own arithmetic (16,797,720 plus 2,466,762) supports 19.26m [6][1]. A strategic holder at over 20% that keeps buying is the kind of holder move the special situations list watches, so it is reported back for that list rather than as a research pick.
- Other substantial holders: Salter Brothers Emerging Companies, up from 8.51% (November 2025) through 9.73% and 10.76% to 12.04% (9,902,921 shares, 28 August 2026); the CEO Justin Hales's family fund 7.33%. Perennial Value Management ceased on 13 March 2026 and Regal on 5 June 2026 [1][8].
- Directors bought on market through 2026, including Justin Hales (294,118 shares for A$102,983, about A$0.35, 21 September 2026), Michael Rosenbaum (100,000 for A$34,717, 28 August 2026), Andrew McEvoy, John Myler and Li Ping Xue [9].
- The facts a deep dive would start from: FY26 EBITDA A$0.3m (first half a A$3.2m loss, second half a A$3.5m profit), statutory loss A$0.8m, cash A$10.0m at 30 June and A$11.3m at 30 September 2026 with no debt, but A$21.6m of trade payables (including money owed to van owners) and A$5.9m of bookings paid in advance; intangibles of A$48.4m exceed equity of A$41.3m, so net tangible assets are negative. Forward bookings were A$21.1m at 30 September 2026, 17% below a year earlier [1][2][10].
- Next dated events: AGM on 11 November 2026 [11]; December quarter cash flow report about late January 2027 (inferred from 30 January 2026); 1H27 result about 26 February 2027 (inferred from 26 February 2026) [4].
