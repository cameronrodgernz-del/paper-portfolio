# hipages Group (ASX:HPG): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 5 points, #31 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. An online home improvement marketplace with subscription software for trade businesses (hipages in Australia, Builderscrack in New Zealand) and, from 30 April 2026, 51% of Viz Insurance. FY26 (year to 30 June 2026) revenue A$90.6m, of which A$88.6m (98%) recurring [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. PwC's opinion on the FY26 accounts is unmodified, on a going-concern basis [3]. Cash and funds on deposit A$34.2m, borrowings A$0.2m [1].
- Too thin to trade? Yes. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$3,803 a day (close times volume, data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz; lowest A$18, highest A$27,662; 5 of the 20 sessions above A$10,000) [9]. Longer windows: A$7,538 over 40 sessions and A$10,390 over 60 sessions, so trading has thinned since the August result. Under rulebook section 3 the largest position would be two sessions at 10% of the 20-session median, about A$761, below the A$1,500 smallest ASX order. The sheet ends here.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No holder over 50%, and no approach or review announced. The largest holder, News Pty Limited (News Corp), went from 27.31% to 29.06% by buying 2,400,000 shares on market for A$3,240,000 (A$1.35 a share) on 16 December 2025 [4]. A strategic holder buying towards 30% is noted in the report back, but it does not meet this test. The company is buying back up to 13,690,391 shares (10%) on market to 13 May 2027; 778,710 had been bought by 5 November 2026 [5].

Facts gathered before the liquidity check ended the sheet, kept for the record:
- Guidance cut at the AGM on 5 November 2026: "economic conditions and consumer confidence have materially deteriorated", so FY27 revenue growth, EBITDA margin and free cash flow are now expected at the lower end of the August ranges (revenue growth 9% to 11%, EBITDA margin 25% to 27%, free cash flow A$11m to A$13m) [6][2].
- Capitalised development: A$14.5m was capitalised in FY26 (A$14.4m in FY25) against A$14.6m of amortisation of past development (A$15.1m in FY25), so statutory profit before tax is not flattered on a net basis, but EBITDA (A$22.9m) is lifted by the full A$14.5m [1][3].
- Statutory profit after tax of A$15.0m includes A$8.9m from recognising past tax losses and R&D offsets for the first time; the company's pro-forma profit excluding it was A$6.1m [1].
- Directors: no on-market buys or sells in the last 12 months; the notices are rights grants and exercises [7].

## 7. Grade
C, no deep dive: a kill fact applies (too thin to trade, section 1). The same liquidity limit would rule out News Corp's 29% stake as a special situation trade.
