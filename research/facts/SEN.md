# Senetas Corporation (ASX:SEN): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 5 points, #36 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. Senetas designs and sells high-assurance network encryption hardware and software (FY26 revenue A$20.2m, gross margin 82%), sold worldwide through Thales. It also holds, through its 62.1%-owned subsidiary VGM Aust Holdings, about 1.6% of Menlo Security Inc., the private US company that bought its Votiro business in February 2025 [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. Grant Thornton's opinion on the FY26 accounts (year to 30 June 2026) is unmodified [1].
- Too thin to trade? No, narrowly. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$11,333 a day (all 20 sessions traded; 9 were below A$10,000; lowest A$133), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz [16]. Under rulebook section 3 a position could be no larger than about A$2,270.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. The largest holders are Madison Park LLC (12.79%) and the chairman Francis Galbally's entities (13.1%) [1]. The board returns surplus cash by capital reductions (section 4); no approach or review has been announced.

Whether the gap between statutory and underlying profit recurs: the VGM revaluation (A$3.65m gain in FY26) and the non-cash interest on the VGM loan recur every period, in either direction, and are not cash. FY25's A$27.8m profit was the one-off gain on selling Votiro, which is why profit "more than halved". The underlying encryption business is close to break-even: EBITDA A$1.24m, and A$0.42m after capital spending and lease payments.

What the headline numbers hide:
- One distributor. Thales took A$17.17m of the A$20.17m revenue (85%) and has exclusivity everywhere except Australia and New Zealand [1]. Gross margin fell from 87% to 82% as Thales built inventory [2].
- Currency. Almost 90% of revenue is earned in US dollars [2].
- Rising costs. FY27 R&D is to rise by A$1.0m to A$1.5m (certifications and a new AI-governance product not expected to earn revenue in the short to medium term), against FY26 underlying EBITDA of A$1.24m [2].
- The Menlo stake is the larger asset. VGM's Menlo shares are independently valued at US$21.36m (A$31.04m); Senetas's equity and loan in VGM are worth A$21.0m of that. No Menlo sale or listing has been announced; management "continue[s] to expect a transaction is likely in the next 12 to 24 months" [1][2].
- Deferred revenue. Contract liabilities of A$9.89m are maintenance paid upfront and still to be delivered [1].

## 3. Guidance and record
- FY27 (28 Aug 2026): operating revenue "to grow broadly in line with FY2026", gross margins "in line with historic levels", R&D up A$1.0m to A$1.5m [2]. The AGM on 13 Nov 2026 is the next chance to update it.
- FY26: "we expect the Senetas operating business revenue and profit to grow in FY2026" (29 Aug 2025) [5]; repeated at the half, "we expect both revenue and profit will grow" over the next 12 months, with A$1.3m of delayed Middle East sales expected in the March quarter (27 Feb 2026) [4]. Delivered: revenue +4.7%, underlying EBITDA down from A$1.96m to A$1.24m. Revenue met, profit missed.
- FY25: "expecting FY2025 sales to be slightly below FY2024" (4 Jul 2025) [9]; delivered A$19.25m against A$21.58m, down 11% [5].

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: is the encryption business worth more than the A$1m or so the market leaves for it after Senetas's cash and the Menlo stake, and when does the Menlo stake turn into cash that is handed back? The business is at break-even after spending (A$0.42m of pre-tax cash earnings in FY26, A$1.27m in FY25), 85% of its sales go through Thales, and FY27 R&D rises by A$1m to A$1.5m.
- How the market prices this kind of business: the operating business on its earnings; the cash at face value, because the board has twice returned surplus cash; and the Menlo stake only through a sale or listing of Menlo, which is the mechanism, followed by a capital return. Multiples for enterprise value over pre-tax cash earnings after development spending and leases, from the desk's earlier work on FY26 filings (research/facts/KYP.md, sources listed there): hipages 7.6 times (a business without growth), Objective Corporation 21.3 times (durable growth). Senetas's revenue has gone from A$21.58m (FY24) to A$19.25m and A$20.17m, so the no-growth multiple applies.
- If it goes our way: Menlo is sold or listed at its June 2026 value and Senetas's A$21.0m comes back to holders; Senetas's own cash, A$18.58m less VGM's A$0.82m less the A$3.5m return, is A$14.26m; the encryption business recovers to FY25's pre-tax cash earnings of A$1.27m (EBITDA A$1.96m less A$0.34m capital spending and A$0.34m lease payments), at hipages' 7.6 times, A$9.67m. Total A$44.93m on 16,577,389 shares = A$2.71 a share. (At Objective's 21.3 times the business would add A$27.1m, A$3.76 a share.)
- If it goes against us: no Menlo transaction in the next two years, and the stake is worth half its book value, A$10.5m (the desk's judgement for an unlisted minority stake held through a subsidiary, with no exit date); FY27's extra R&D takes the encryption business to nil cash earnings, counted at nothing; cash A$14.26m. Total A$24.76m, A$1.49 a share.
- Only now the price: A$2.20 (Norgate close 30 Oct 2026, and the ASX's last trade on 6 Nov). Gap if it goes our way: 2.71 / 2.20 - 1 = +23%. Gap if it goes against us: 1.49 / 2.20 - 1 = -32%. At A$2.20 the market value (A$36.5m) is about Senetas's own cash plus the Menlo stake at book; net tangible assets after the August return are about A$2.28 a share.

## 7. Grade
C, no deep dive: the gap if it goes our way is +23%, under 25%. Priced on what its encryption business earns, plus its cash and the Menlo stake at book, Senetas sits about where the market has it. The case changes only if Menlo is sold or listed above its June 2026 value, or the encryption business grows its earnings enough to earn a growth multiple (Objective's would give A$3.76, +71%). Watch for a Menlo transaction (management expects one within 12 to 24 months) and the H1 FY27 result (about 27 Feb 2027).
