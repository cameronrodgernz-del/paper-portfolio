# BSA Limited (ASX:BSA): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 6 points, #12 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. BSA installs and maintains telecommunications, smart metering and EV charging equipment; Foxtel is its anchor customer, with a contract extended to October 2029 [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? Yes, going-concern doubt. BDO's report on the June 2026 accounts (20 August 2026) carries a "Material uncertainty related to going concern" paragraph: note 2 "describes the events and/or conditions which give rise to the existence of a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt about the group's ability to continue as a going concern". The opinion itself is not modified [1]. The directors give the reason as "the unfavorable outcome in the nbn tender in prior year and the risk that the Group may be unprofitable due to reduced revenues" [1]. The same material uncertainty appeared in the December 2025 half-year review [3]. The shares were halted on 24 September 2026 and suspended at BSA's request from 28 September, then reinstated on 1 October "following the release by BSA of an announcement regarding a proposed material acquisition" (Eonetics) [15][4].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$20,636 a day, counting the two sessions with no trades as zero (A$32,013 over the last 20 days that traded), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No takeover of BSA. BSA is itself buying Eonetics Pty Ltd (tethered drones and remote infrastructure) for 50,000,000 new BSA shares at a deemed A$0.35, 24,500,000 options (A$0.40 to A$1.00) and 30,000,000 performance shares that convert on Eonetics revenue milestones of A$5m, A$7.5m and A$10m between 2027 and 2029 [4][5]. The sellers would hold about 39.9% of the 125,300,261 shares on issue after completion; no seller is expected to exceed 19.9% [4]. Shareholders vote on 25 November 2026 [6].

The sheet ends here on the going-concern kill.

For the record (facts only, no valuation):
- The business has shrunk to a fraction of its old size. Revenue and other income fell from A$287.0m in FY25 to A$32.1m in FY26 after the nbn contract ended on 30 September 2025. FY26 profit of A$4.3m "included non-recurring transition-out payments" from nbn [1]. The largest customer was 37% of FY26 revenue and the next 35% [1].
- September 2026 quarter: revenue A$4.4m (A$17.1m a year earlier), underlying EBITDA a loss of A$0.6m before a A$1.0m share-based payment charge, operating cash outflow A$1.7m, net cash A$14.6m at 30 September (A$16.4m at 30 June), no borrowings [7]. Shares on issue 80,300,261 [8].
- The chair, David Geraghty, has been buying on market since June 2026: about 5.25 million shares for about A$1.60m in the notices read, at A$0.24 to A$0.32 a share, including 2,800,000 shares on 21 July 2026 for A$896,000 (A$0.32) [9]. The buys are held through Roologic Pty Ltd, a company controlled by his spouse [9], which became a substantial holder at 6.48% on 21 July 2026 and held 7.51% (5,660,246 shares) by 5 October 2026 [10][11]. After the 21 October 2026 notice the chair's interests were 150,000 shares directly, 5,967,246 shares through Roologic (7.4% of 80.3m shares) and, through Mandarin Rock Pty Ltd, 2,000,000 options at A$0.50 and 2,000,000 at A$0.75, expiring May 2029 [9]. On 9 June 2026 BSA agreed to lend a company controlled by the chair up to A$2,000,000, unsecured at 10%, solely to buy BSA shares [12].
- Director Daniel Raihani's 7 Enterprises Pty Ltd holds 22.41% (18,000,000 shares) after converting 3,000,000 performance rights on 6 October 2026 [13].
- The AGM on 25 November 2026 carries a conditional spill resolution (the 2025 remuneration report drew a first strike), the Eonetics approvals (Resolutions 7 to 9) and re-insertion of the proportional takeover provisions [6].
- On 28 October 2026 BSA withdrew its 21 October Eonetics investor presentation and released a replacement [14].

## 7. Grade
C, on a named kill fact: the auditor's material uncertainty on going concern in the FY26 accounts. Sections 2 to 6 were not written, as the template requires once a kill check answer is yes. The next dated events are the AGM and Eonetics vote on 25 November 2026 [6], the December 2026 quarterly (about 30 January 2027, inferred from 30 January 2026) and the half-year accounts (about 20 February 2027, inferred from 20 February 2026), where the auditor's going-concern wording will show again. It is not a special situations case on the rules: BSA is the buyer, not the target, and no holder is over 50%.
