## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. Beacon mines gold near Coolgardie in Western Australia and owns the Jaurdi mill (about 1.05 Mtpa, being expanded). Since MacPhersons Reward finished in February 2026, its main ore source is the Iguana deposit at Lady Ida, hauled 43km to Jaurdi [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. Stantons' opinion on the June 2026 accounts is unmodified; the key audit matter is the accounting for the Lady Ida joint venture [1].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the last 20 sessions in the file (2 to 30 October 2026) was A$86,803 a day (lowest A$8,967; 19 of 20 sessions above A$10,000), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz [24].
- On Cam's excluded list? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries (Cam confirmed the list on 8 Nov 2026).
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. The largest holder is Colin Petroulas with 23.26%, then the chair and managing director Graham McGarry with 15.46% and director Geoffrey Greenhill with 6.40% [1]. Mr Petroulas has gone from 19.99% (June 2025) to 22.10% (2 Feb 2026) and 23.26% (3 Aug 2026); his forms leave the details of the changes blank [14][15]. A holder building past 20% is noted in the report back, but it does not meet this test. No approach or review has been announced.

FY25 had no such items; its loss came from A$28.3m of depreciation as MacPhersons was written off [1].

What the headline numbers hide:
- Beacon only owns half of its main ore source for now. Lady Ida belongs to Lamerton and Geoda, companies controlled by the chair Graham McGarry and director Geoffrey Greenhill. Under the earn-in, Beacon took a 50% share of the joint venture at first gold on 1 April 2026 (and 25% of the tenements), rises to 50% of the tenements at 36,250oz and takes 100% once 72,500oz have been recovered, when a 4% net smelter royalty is granted to the two companies [1][6]. In the September quarter Beacon's share was 3,303oz of the 6,250oz produced [2]. Lady Ida produced about 10,460oz to 30 Sep 2026 (4,566oz in the June quarter and about 5,890oz in September), so about 62,000oz remain before Beacon owns it outright [1][2].
- The reserve grade against the grade being mined. The Iguana ore reserve is 11.0Mt at 1.20 g/t for 430,200oz (99% Probable, 100% basis, at A$5,500/oz), and the pre-feasibility study expects 379,190oz over 112 months of milling from August 2026 at an all-in cost of A$3,283/oz [6]. In the September quarter the mill ran at 0.85 g/t, and the Iguana ore recovered about 0.74 g/t (5,890oz from about 248,700t); all-in sustaining cost was A$4,531/oz (June quarter A$5,448, FY26 A$4,285) [2].
- Gold price. Realised A$5,998/oz in the September quarter, against A$6,371 in June and A$7,045 in March; no hedges [2][7]. At A$5,998 the September quarter margin over all-in cost was A$1,467/oz.
- Mine life and other ore. Iguana is the only ore reserve; about 9 years at the study's rate. Stockpiles at 30 Sep 2026 were 1,143,000t holding 21,430oz, including 511,000t (6,500oz) of low-grade Iguana material not planned for processing [2].
- Rehabilitation provisions of A$24.0m (non-current) sit on the balance sheet [1].
- Related-party spending: McVerde Minerals (equipment hire and carbon stripping, A$1.43m), Mangelsdorf Engineering (A$0.40m) and Kinetiq Solutions (A$0.60m) are connected to Mr McGarry or Mr Greenhill [1].

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: once the expanded mill runs at 1.2 Mtpa, does Iguana deliver the study's grade and cost (1.20 g/t, about 88% recovery, all-in A$3,283/oz at A$5,500), or does it stay near the September quarter's 0.74 g/t recovered and A$4,531/oz? That decides whether Beacon's share of Iguana is worth about A$400m or about A$110m.
- How the market prices it: gold producers are priced on the after-tax cash flow from their reserves (net asset value). The mechanism is the quarterly production reports: two quarters at the study's grade would show it.
- Common terms: gold at A$6,000/oz (September quarter realised A$5,998); 30% tax; 10% discount rate, mid-year; Beacon's share is 50% of Lady Ida ounces until 72,500oz (about 62,000oz to go at 30 Sep 2026), then 100% less the 4% royalty. Balance sheet at 30 Sep 2026: cash A$27.69m, less equipment finance A$7.89m, plus 1,546oz at the Perth Mint (A$9.28m), less the FY26 tax still owed (A$9.50m less A$5.63m paid, A$3.87m), less rehabilitation provisions A$23.99m: A$1.22m. Listed options exercised for A$3.15m; shares 119.00m. Stockpiles outside Iguana are counted at nil.
- If it goes our way: the study holds. At A$6,000 the study's figures are operating cost A$2,914/oz, royalties A$396/oz (of which the 4% royalty A$217), capital A$7/oz: all-in A$3,316/oz, 40,628oz a year. Phase one (1.53 years): Beacon's half of 40,628oz at A$6,000 less A$3,099 (no 4% royalty yet) is A$58.9m a year before tax. Phase two (7.71 years): 40,628oz at A$6,000 less A$3,316, A$109.0m a year. Less A$3m a year of corporate and exploration cost, less tax, discounted at 10%: A$405.9m. Plus A$1.22m and A$3.15m: A$410.2m on 119.00m shares, A$3.45 a share. Adding the unlisted options at their strikes changes it by about 1c.
- If it goes against us: the September quarter repeats. Iguana recovers 0.737 g/t on about 0.99 Mtpa (23,572oz a year), so the 10.90Mt left in the reserve gives 258,214oz, at A$4,531/oz all-in. Phase one (2.63 years): Beacon's half at A$1,469/oz margin, A$17.3m a year. Phase two (8.32 years): 100% at A$1,229/oz after the 4% royalty, A$29.0m a year. Less A$2m a year of exploration, less tax, discounted at 10%: A$109.3m. Plus A$4.37m: A$113.7m on 119.00m shares, A$0.96 a share.
- Gold price: A$500/oz either way moves the "our way" figure by about A$0.61 a share (A$2.84 at A$5,500, A$4.06 at A$6,500).
- Price: A$2.73 (last trade on 6 Nov 2026, ASX company data; A$2.50 the session before and A$2.64 at the 30 Oct close), market value A$317.7m. Gap if it goes our way: 3.45 / 2.73 - 1 = +26%. Gap if it goes against us: 0.96 / 2.73 - 1 = -65%. The price rose 9% on 6 November with no company announcement; at A$2.50 the gap would be +38%.

## 7. Grade
B, deep dive when its event is close. The gap if it goes our way is 26%, at or above 25% but under 40%. The answer needs the events themselves: the December quarter update (about 15 Jan 2027) and the March quarter (about 16 Apr 2027) report the head grade, recovery and all-in cost for the expanded mill on Iguana ore, so the deep dive belongs just before the January update. Not a value pick: the value if it goes against us is 65% below the price. A deep dive would also need to read the earn-in agreement itself, since the insiders who own the other half of Lady Ida also run Beacon.
