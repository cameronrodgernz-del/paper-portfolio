# Auric Mining (ASX:AWJ): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-02 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 6 points, #9 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-01).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

Auric is a small Western Australian gold company near Widgiemooltha and Coolgardie. It made its money so far from a
share of the Jeffreys Find joint venture (finished March 2026) and from mining the small Munda Starter Pit and having
the ore milled by others (finished March 2026). It now plans one larger open pit at Munda feeding the Burbanks mill,
which it bought in 2025 and must rebuild. Its year ends 31 December.

## 1. Kill check
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? A reading, not a kill. Auric has no mine or mill
  running today and no Ore Reserve; first gold from the new project is targeted for March 2028. But it has A$33m of cash,
  a scoping study, 301,000 ounces of resources and the Burbanks mill site, so I have treated it as a business with
  assets to value.
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. The 2025 audit opinion is unmodified;
  William Buck's review of the June 2026 half found nothing to report. One court matter: Estrella Resources issued a
  writ on 30 April 2025 over lithium rights and a lithium royalty on the Munda lease (M15/87); Auric is defending it and
  says it doesn't affect gold mining (June 2026 half-year report).
- Too thin to trade? No, but close to thin. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$40,808
  (volume times close, data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz; lowest session A$3,487). Two sessions at 10% of that
  median is about A$8,200, enough for a 2.5% position (about A$2,200) and above the A$1,500 smallest order.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. The largest holder is the
  Markoff family at 10.56%.

Nothing ends the sheet.

Does the gap between statutory and underlying recur? There is no underlying figure, but the point is the opposite: none
of the profit recurs. The Starter Pit (8,886 ounces in total at an all-in sustaining cost of A$2,857 an ounce) and
Jeffreys Find (final payment A$2.2m in March 2026) are both finished. The ranking's "1.8 times earnings" uses earnings
that will not come back until the new project produces.

What the headline numbers hide:
- The "net cash about 88% of market value" uses the A$33m balance before the Union Jack payment and the tax bill. After
  both, about A$19.2m is left, while the Appendix 5B shows A$2.7m a quarter going on exploration.
- The commodity price assumption: the scoping study's base case is A$5,750 an ounce, which it called about the spot
  price on 27 July 2026, with A$5,250 and A$6,250 cases; the Starter Pit sold at an average of A$7,178. Study EBITDA is
  A$368m, A$437m and A$506m at the three prices.
- Mine life and reserves: a single Munda pit, 2.77Mt at 1.75 g/t for 155,800 ounces mined (93.1% Indicated resource,
  the rest Inferred), processed over 56 months for 141,300 ounces recovered. No Ore Reserve yet; the company said one
  was "expected to be announced in October 2026" (quarterly, 15 Oct 2026), and none had been lodged by 1 November. Total
  resources after Union Jack: 7.5Mt at 1.25 g/t for 301,000 ounces, the Union Jack 102,000 ounces all Inferred.
- Funding: pre-production capital A$138m (Burbanks rebuild A$94.3m, plus or minus 15%; owner's costs A$35.8m, plus or
  minus 25%; Munda pit A$7.5m) and peak funding A$145m. The study gives no net present value and says funding "may only
  be available on terms that may be dilutive".
- Disclosure record: on 13 April 2026 Auric withdrew a production target from a roadshow presentation because it did not
  meet Listing Rule 5.16, and on 5 June 2026 it re-issued its Munda resource announcement at ASX's request. Its 30 March
  2026 results release labelled year-end cash (A$6.5m) as "free cash flow".

## 3. Guidance and record
- No earnings or production guidance. The plan, as stated on 27 July and 15 October 2026: approvals in early 2027,
  Burbanks construction after that, mining from about December 2027, first gold about March 2028.
- Timing has moved once: the April 2026 roadshow presentation said Burbanks would process ore from the December quarter
  of 2027 and mining at Munda and Lindsay's would start in the September or December quarter of 2027; the July 2026
  study moved first gold to March 2028 and covers Munda only. Lindsay's is held up: forfeiture applications were lodged
  against some of its leases before transfer, and the Warden's Court proceedings were still going in the September 2026
  quarter (4 tenements have transferred).
- The Ore Reserve promised for October 2026 is late.
- Earlier record: the 2025 annual report says the Starter Pit sold at about A$6,200 an ounce against a prefeasibility
  assumption of A$3,500, so the past profit owed much to the gold price.

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: can Auric fund the A$145m Munda and Burbanks build without new shares taking most of the project's value
  from existing holders, and does the Ore Reserve support the 56-month plan at a gold price near A$5,750?
- If it goes our way: the project goes ahead as studied and holders keep half its value after funding. The study gives
  no present value, so this is the desk's own calculation from the study's yearly table: EBITDA by year (A$33.4m, 76.9m,
  153.8m, 149.2m, 23.5m), less A$5m a year of head office cost, sustaining capital of A$26.3m and tax at 30% after
  depreciating A$164.3m of capital, less A$138m built in 2027 and A$8.1m of working capital, discounted at 8%: A$95m.
  Half is A$47.6m. Add A$19.2m of cash left after Union Jack and the tax bill: A$66.8m / 200.4m shares (including the
  Union Jack shares) = A$0.33. (At A$5,250 the same method gives A$55m; at A$6,250, A$133m.)
- If it goes against us: the project can't be funded on useful terms and is worth nothing beyond the cash: A$19.2m /
  200.4m = A$0.096 (book NTA of 26c includes A$15.6m of capitalised exploration and the mill, which would be worth less).
- Only now the price: A$0.23 (close 30 October 2026). Gap if it goes our way: 0.333 / 0.23 - 1 = +45%. Gap if it goes
  against us: 0.096 / 0.23 - 1 = -58%.

## 7. Grade
B, deep dive when its event is close. The gap if it goes our way is 45%, but the answer needs the event itself: the
funding package, which should follow the early-2027 approvals and may land after 2 May 2027. The late Ore Reserve is the
first marker and is due any day.
