## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No, on the rule used for earlier sheets: a trust that owns and runs real property counts as a business. CDP's sole investment is a 50% interest in Westfield Carindale, Brisbane, which includes the Millennium Boulevard office and retail centre; it holds no listed securities or loans. It is managed by Scentre Group, which owns the other 50% [1][2]. Cam may want to rule on whether the word "trust" in this check is meant to catch property trusts.
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. The auditor's opinion on the FY26 accounts is clean, with no material uncertainty or emphasis of matter [1].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 1 to 30 October 2026 was A$48,698 a day (lowest A$5; six sessions under A$10,000), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? Yes. Scentre Management Limited, as responsible entity of Scentre Group Trust 1, holds more than two-thirds of the units and has been buying more. Its notice of 20 April 2026 shows it went from 55,386,192 units (66.94%, notice of 3 March 2025) to 56,230,262 units (67.96%) through on-market purchases and special crossings between 12 March and 17 April 2026, at A$5.29 to A$5.50 a unit (including crossings of 450,000 units at A$5.425 on 19 March and 240,000 units at A$5.38 on 14 April) [3][4]. The annual report shows 56,581,540 units at 30 June 2026, 68.39% of the 82,737,213 on issue, so the buying continued below the next notice threshold [1]. Scentre Management Limited is also CDP's own responsible entity, and Scentre owns the other half of the centre [1][5].

That is a holder over 50% making moves, so the sheet ends here and the trust goes to the special situations watch list.

For the watch list:
- Who can move the price: Scentre, at 68.4%. A holder of 19% or more may buy 3% every six months without a bid (the "creep" exception in the Corporations Act); a full takeover or a trust scheme would need an announcement. The last notice was 20 April 2026; another is due when Scentre reaches 68.96% [3].
- What Scentre paid: A$5.29 to A$5.50 a unit in March and April 2026 [3].
- The other substantial holder, Renaissance Property Securities, fell from 6.17% (September 2021) to 5.09% (22 July 2026 notice, filed 7 September 2026) [6].
- The facts a deep dive would start from (not graded here): FY26 funds from operations A$32.3m (39.04c a unit), statutory profit A$59.0m including a A$25.6m revaluation gain, distribution 29.883c (up 5.0%), NTA A$7.20 a unit, gearing 25.3%, A$205.6m drawn on a A$230m facility due May 2032, the centre valued at A$1,628m (CDP's share A$814m) at 30 June 2026, occupancy 99.9%. FY27 distribution guidance is 31.38c (up 5.0%) [1][2]. AFFO (after maintenance capital, leasing incentives and fees) was not worked; the template's AFFO method would be the deep dive's first job.
- News since the result: on 5 November 2026 the trust noted administrators had been appointed to David Jones. The David Jones store at Westfield Carindale is 7,635 square metres, about 1.8% of CDP's share of gross rent and 3.4% of FFO, at A$285 a square metre against the centre's average of A$870; rent is paid to 30 September 2026 and CDP's share of a bank guarantee is about A$0.74m (about 8 months' rent). FY27 distribution guidance of 31.38c was reaffirmed [5].
- Next dated events: the December 2026 half-year distribution notice (about 22 December, inferred from 22 December 2025) [7]; the 1H27 result about 24 February 2027 (inferred from 24 February 2026; the ranking shows 24 February 2027) [8]; any further Scentre notice (no date); the David Jones administrators' decision on the lease (no date).
