# EVZ Limited (ASX:EVZ): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 4 points, #56 of 195 ASX names in reports/research_rank.csv (2026-11-08).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

EVZ is a small Melbourne-based engineering contractor with two divisions. Energy & Resources (FY26 revenue A$77.3m, 63%) is
Brockman Engineering (steel tanks, fuel terminals, water storage, shutdown work) and TSF Power (gas engine and standby power
service, parts and rebuilds). Building Products (A$45.8m, 37%) is Syfon Systems (syphonic roof drainage in Australia and
Southeast Asia) and Tank Industries (panel tanks, recently for a data centre). 94% of revenue is Australian [1].

## 1. Kill check
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. An operating contractor with A$123.1m of FY26 revenue [1].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. Grant Thornton's opinion on the FY26
  accounts is unmodified [1].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$73,685 a day (volume times
  close, data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz; lowest session A$1,423, highest A$191,468). Two sessions at 10% of that
  median is about A$14,700 [20].
- On Cam's excluded list? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no companies; Cam confirmed on 8 Nov 2026 there is nothing to
  avoid at the outset. EVZ is not in gaming, wagering or racing.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. The largest holder is Tiga
  Trading (Alex Waislitz) at 14.32%, and it has been selling (section 4) [15]. The company says it is looking at "several"
  acquisitions with "no certainty that any transaction will go ahead" [5]; that is EVZ as a buyer, not a sale.

Nothing ends the sheet.

Does the gap between statutory and underlying profit recur? There is no gap: the company does not adjust its statutory
profit. The only non-cash charge worth noting is share-based pay, 0.36m in FY26 (0.13m in FY25), which recurs and is left in.

What the headline numbers hide:
- Customer concentration has gone up. Three Energy & Resources customers made up 40% of FY26 revenue (23% in FY25), and one
  Building Products customer another 6% [1] note 3. Brockman's largest recent job, the Rio Tinto coastal water supply tanks at
  Parker Point, WA (stages one and two about A$46m), has now finished [5].
- The order book is not growing. "Exceeding $80 million" (Chairman, 25 Aug 2026) and "exceeding $90 million, including recent
  contract wins" (results presentation the same day), then "$80M, stable from the previous report" at 30 Sep 2026 [1][2][5].
- Part of the cash is customers' money. Contract liabilities (cash received ahead of work) were 9.66m against contract
  assets of 4.20m, a net 5.46m that unwinds as jobs finish [1].
- FY26 cash flow was flattered. Receivables fell 3.36m and contract liabilities rose 3.60m; income tax paid was only
  0.15m because carried-forward tax losses (0.80m of tax value at June 2025) were used up in FY26 [1] notes 5, 30. From FY27
  EVZ pays full cash tax. The first quarter of FY27 was a 0.4m operating outflow [5].
- Goodwill is 12.07m (Brockman 8.79m, Syfon 3.28m), 32% of net assets [1] note 13.
- Dilution: about 6.15m performance rights remain (8,688,500 after the 7 Aug 2026 grants, less 2,538,500 converted on
  1 Sep 2026), 4.7% of shares on issue [19]. The AGM also asks for a 10% placement facility, and management says it is
  working on acquisitions [5][6].
- Bank guarantees of 6.85m given to customers sit off the balance sheet [1] note 24.

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: is FY26's A$3.9m profit a new base now that the Rio Tinto tank job is finished, three customers made up 40%
  of revenue and the order book has stopped growing at about A$80m, or was FY26 a peak? The 1H27 guidance (about 20 Nov 2026)
  and the 1H27 accounts (about 20 Feb 2027, with segment EBITDA and the customer split) should show it.
- How the market prices it, and the mechanism: small engineering and fabrication contractors are priced on earnings. The
  named peers are the profitable listed contractors and fabricators in the ASX company data (prices of 6 Nov 2026): Civmec
  (CVL) 15.9 times trailing earnings, Korvest (KOV) 15.3, Verbrec (VBC) 19.1, Mayfield (MYG) 20.8; median 17.5. Saunders
  International (SND), the closest match in steel tanks and fuel terminals, lost A$0.9m in FY26 so has no multiple [20]. The
  price would get there through half-year results that show profit holding without the Rio Tinto job, and the dividend.
- Deductions used throughout: cash of A$17.0m (A$17.7m at 30 Sep 2026 less the 0.55c dividend paid on 124.04m shares on
  21 Oct 2026) and no debt. Per-share values use 130.19m shares (124.04m on issue plus all 6.15m performance rights).
- If it goes our way: FY26 profit holds. Statutory profit A$3.866m less after-tax interest income (0.288m x 0.7 = 0.201m) is
  A$3.664m from the business. 3.664 x 17.5 = A$64.1m, plus cash A$17.0m = A$81.2m. A$81.2m / 130.19m = A$0.62.
- If it goes against us: profit falls back to its five-year average, (0.895 + 1.446 + 2.139 + 1.235 + 3.866) / 5 = A$1.916m
  (five-year table in [1]), at the same 17.5 times = A$33.5m; cash less the net customer prepayments (17.0 - 5.46 = A$11.6m)
  gives A$45.1m. A$45.1m / 130.19m = A$0.35.
- Check on the multiple: at the two fabricators alone (Civmec and Korvest, average 15.6 times) the our-way value is A$0.57;
  at 12 times it would be A$0.48.
- Only now the price: A$0.40, the last trade in the ASX company data fetched 7 Nov 2026 (no shares traded on 6 Nov; the
  Norgate close on 30 Oct was A$0.38). Market value A$49.6m on 124.04m shares; less cash, about 3.6 times FY26 EBITDA and
  8.9 times FY26 profit from the business. The 12-month high was A$0.77 and the low A$0.19 [20]. Gap if it goes our way:
  0.62 / 0.40 - 1 = +56%. Gap if it goes against us: 0.35 / 0.40 - 1 = -13%. On shares on issue only (no rights): +64% and -9%.

## 7. Grade
A, deep dive now. The gap if it goes our way is +56% on the peer median (+43% even at the fabricators' 15.6 times), above 40%.
The question can be worked from filings (segment EBITDA, the major-customer note, backlog by division in the presentations,
working capital and tax), and the 1H27 guidance (about 20 Nov 2026) and result (about 20 Feb 2027), both inside six months,
should show whether profit held after the Rio Tinto job. Not a value pick: the against value is 13% below the price on the
diluted share count (9% below on shares on issue alone, so it is close). The deep dive should also weigh the selling by the
Chairman (A$0.53) and Tiga (A$0.50 and A$0.70) against Wilson Asset Management's buying (A$0.49 to A$0.70), and the risk that
the 10% placement facility funds an acquisition with new shares.
