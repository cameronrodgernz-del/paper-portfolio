## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. Mānuka honey producer and brand owner: own apiaries and forests in New Zealand, sales through Greater China, North America, the rest of Asia, Australia and New Zealand, and Europe and the Middle East. FY26 (to 30 Jun 2026) revenue NZ$213.0m, profit after tax NZ$7.7m [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. Audited by KPMG; the directors conclude it is a going concern, with net cash of NZ$0.5m after a NZ$40.5m capital raise in May 2026 and a new NZ$43.9m bank facility to September 2028 [1][2].
- Too thin to trade? Yes. The NZX price feed has no volumes, so this uses a stated secondary source: Yahoo Finance daily data for CVT.NZ (query1.finance.yahoo.com chart API, fetched 2 Nov 2026). Median value traded over the 20 sessions 2 to 30 October 2026 was NZ$4,209 a day (lowest NZ$81, highest NZ$28,026; five of the 20 sessions above NZ$12,500). Investing.com's daily volumes for the same weeks match (for example 34,180 shares on 19 Oct 2026, about NZ$28,000 at the NZ$0.82 close) [3]. That is a third of the NZ$12,500 limit. Under rulebook section 3 the largest position would be two sessions at 10% of the median, about NZ$842, below the NZ$2,500 smallest NZX order. Over 40 sessions (4 Sep to 30 Oct 2026) the median is NZ$6,485, still under the limit. The sheet ends here.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No holder is over 50%, but the register and board have changed hands this year, which belongs on the special situations watch list:
  - F&N Ventures (Fraser and Neave, about 69.66% owned by Thai Beverage) took 19.99% (25,954,823 shares) on 18 May 2026: 12.8m rights offer shortfall shares as part-underwriter at NZ$0.65 and a 13.2m share placement at NZ$0.80 [4][5]. 19.99% is just under the 20% Takeovers Code threshold.
  - PHC Investments became substantial on 16 Apr 2026 (13.06%, mostly an NZ$5.82m crossing at NZ$0.68 from Li Wang, who held 12.13%), now 11.74%; on 21 Jul 2026 it put 7,620,771 shares on bare trust for Andrew Barclay's Securities Holdings for NZ$5.18m while keeping the votes [6][7][8][9]. China Resources Enterprise sold its 6.25% at NZ$0.68 on 16 Apr 2026 [10]. Kauri NZ Investment holds 10.15% [11].
  - At the 28 Oct 2026 annual meeting PHC's resolutions removed independent directors Bob Major (70.4% for) and Greg Barclay (65.6% for); a resolution to remove the Chair, Bridget Coates, failed (45.7% for). F&N's nominee Kosit Suksingha (a ThaiBev executive) was elected with 68.1%. The board is now four non-independent and two independent directors [12][13][14].

What else was found before the sheet ended (for the record, not graded):
- The ranking's "7.7 times earnings" uses NZX's earnings per share of 9.78c, which is on the weighted share count before the May 2026 raise. On the 129,855,365 shares now on issue, FY26 profit is 5.96c a share, about 12.7 times at the NZ$0.755 close of 30 Oct 2026 [1][15]. FY26 tax was NZ$0.4m on NZ$8.2m of pre-tax profit [1].
- FY26 normalised EBIT guidance of about NZ$14.3m was confirmed at the H1 result (23 Feb 2026) and raised to about NZ$15.5m on 16 Mar 2026; the company reports normalised EBIT of NZ$16.4m, above guidance, and statutory operating profit of NZ$14.0m [16][1].
- An unrecognised contingent asset: claims for refunds of US import tariffs paid in FY26, amount not stated [1].
- On-market sells by the former CFO, Nigel Greenwood: 90,600 shares for NZ$56,408 (11 to 17 Feb 2026) and 112,903 for NZ$74,594 (10 Mar 2026) [17]. Directors took up rights at NZ$0.65 in May 2026 (not on-market) [18]. Director dealings before 2026 were not listed: the NZX company page served only the 2026 announcements.
