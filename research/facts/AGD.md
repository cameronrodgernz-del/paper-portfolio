# Austral Gold (ASX:AGD): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 4 points, #40 in reports/research_rank.md (run 2026-11-08 on prices to 2026-11-06).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. Austral mines gold and silver at Guanaco (Chile, reprocessing old heap leach pads) and Casposo (Argentina, restarted October 2025), and holds 15.7m shares in Unico Silver (ASX:USL) [1][2]. It reports in US dollars; its shares also trade on the TSX Venture Exchange.
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. BDO's opinion on the December 2025 accounts is unmodified; going concern was a key audit matter, but there is no material uncertainty paragraph [3]. BDO's review of the June 2026 half is unmodified [1].
- Too thin to trade? Yes. Median value traded on the ASX over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$7,082 a day (close times volume, data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz; lowest A$32, highest A$119,356; 9 of the 20 sessions above A$10,000) [16]. The 60-session median is A$23,807, so trading has thinned since September. Under rulebook section 3 the largest position would be two sessions at 10% of the 20-session median, about A$1,416, below the A$1,500 smallest ASX order. Trading on the TSX Venture Exchange (72.5m shares sit on the Canadian register [3]) is not counted. The sheet ends here.
- On Cam's excluded list? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries (Cam confirmed the list on 8 Nov 2026).
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? A holder over 50%, but no move. The chair, Eduardo Elsztain, controls 461,316,134 shares (69.05%) through Inversiones Financieras del Sur (IFISA), Guanaco Capital Holding and his own name; IFISA is the parent entity [3][4]. His only changes in the last 12 months were dilution by share issues (notices of 18 Dec 2025 and 2 Mar 2026) and 37,833 shares received off market at A$0.12 on 29 May 2026 [4][5]. No bid or review has been announced.

Facts gathered before the liquidity check ended the sheet, kept for the record:
- The controlling holder is also a lender and a counterparty. Related-party loans at 9% were US$8.0m of the US$17.6m of debt at 30 September 2026; US$2.9m fell due on 29 January 2026 and US$7.2m on 31 July 2026, and are being renegotiated; US$2.8m was repaid in the September quarter [1][2]. The Casposo toll processing deal with Challenger Gold (ASX:CEL), where Mr Elsztain is also a key manager, ended on 1 October 2026; Challenger's US$1.0m deferred access fee is now payable by 2 April 2029 at the latest, with accrued interest waived [6]. Toll fees were US$6.5m of the June 2026 half's US$66.6m revenue [1].
- One-off gains in the earnings multiple. Calendar 2025 profit of US$14.7m included US$13.0m of fair value gains on the Unico Silver holding and a US$3.7m impairment reversal at Casposo; without them, and adding back a US$0.8m exploration write-off, pre-tax profit was about US$2.6m [7]. The June 2026 half's profit of US$15.5m (before tax US$24.9m) is mainly from operations, with a US$2.3m loss on Unico shares [1].
- Guidance record: Guanaco has missed in each of the last three years. 2024: 15,573 gold equivalent ounces against a revised 17,000 to 18,000 [8]. 2025: Guanaco guidance of 18,000 to 20,000 was cut to 14,000 to 16,000 (July 2025) and 11,000 to 12,000 (October 2025); it made 11,109 [9][10][7]. 2026: group guidance of 26,000 to 30,000 (5 Dec 2025) was raised to 27,000 to 33,000 (30 Jan 2026) and cut to 24,000 to 27,000 on 27 Oct 2026, with Guanaco now 13,000 to 14,000 and Casposo's all-in sustaining cost guidance raised to US$3,000 to US$3,200 per ounce [11][12][2].
- Reserves and permits. Guanaco's reserve (18.1Mt at 0.84 g/t gold, 352koz, at US$2,200/oz) supports a 14-year plan, but 53% of the reserve gold (about 185koz recoverable, in Inesperada and Dumbo) depends on an environmental permit whose application is to be lodged in December 2026; the estimates were prepared by company employees, not independent experts [13][2]. Casposo's reserve (2.1Mt at 1.29 g/t gold and 56 g/t silver) gave about 74 months of life from mid-2025 [3][6].
- Cash: US$20.1m at 30 September 2026 against US$17.6m of debt, net cash US$2.5m; the Unico Silver stake was worth US$8.0m [2].
- Directors' on-market trades in the last 12 months: none. Ben Jarvis bought 250,000 and 150,000 shares at A$0.045 in September and October 2025, just outside the window [14][15]. The only raising in two years was a placement of 46,977,778 shares at A$0.18 on 23 February 2026 (A$8.456m) [3].

## 7. Grade
C, no deep dive: a kill fact applies (too thin to trade, section 1). The 69% controlling holder has made no move, so this is not a special situation either, though the related-party loans and the Challenger settlement are worth noting if trading ever deepens.
