# Kaiser Reef (ASX:KAU): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 7 points, #5 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. Kaiser mines gold at Henty (underground, Tasmania, bought from Catalyst Metals in May 2025) and runs the small Maldon plant in Victoria; the A1 mine is on care and maintenance [1].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. BDO's opinion on the June 2026 accounts is unmodified; the key audit matters are the final Henty acquisition accounting and the recoverability of mine assets [1].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$178,887 a day (lowest day A$15,894), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. The largest holder is Catalyst Metals with 19.6% [1]. The August 2025 "strategic review" covered only the A1 mine, which is now on care and maintenance [1]. The company says it is "in a great position to take advantage of inorganic opportunities" but has announced nothing [2].

The gap between statutory and underlying profit recurs. Impairments of Victorian assets were A$12.7m in FY25 and A$12.3m in FY26 (and A$9.7m in the December 2025 half), and the company's underlying figure adds them back each year [1][3][4].

What the headline numbers hide:
- One asset. Henty produced 25,812oz of the 27,766oz in FY26 and 94% of gold sales [1]. Maldon lost money: its all-in cash flow was minus A$4.6m in each of the June and September 2026 quarters [2][7].
- A one-off gain in the profit. FY26 profit includes an A$8.0m "settlement gain on delivery", booked when gold was handed over to settle the Auramet gold loan and the Catalyst deferred payment, and an A$3.3m fair value loss [1]. The gold loan is now repaid, so the gain won't recur at that size.
- The gold price the value rests on. Henty realised A$6,061/oz in the September 2026 quarter (group A$6,067) and A$6,177/oz in the June quarter; the FY26 average was A$5,875/oz because 6,000oz went to Auramet at A$5,300/oz under call options, closed out in February 2026 [2][7][8]. In March 2026 the company described spot as "currently >A$7,000/oz" [8], so the A$ gold price has come off since then. All production is now unhedged.
- Costs against the price. Henty's all-in sustaining cost (AISC) was A$4,248/oz for FY26, A$5,674/oz in the June 2026 quarter and A$4,991/oz in the September quarter; group AISC was A$5,051/oz in September [2][7]. At A$6,061/oz the September margin over AISC was about A$1,070/oz. Royalties took about 10% of Henty revenue in the September quarter (the Tasmanian rate applied was 4.07%) [2].
- Reserves and mine life. Henty Probable Ore Reserve 1.91Mt at 3.22g/t for 198koz, estimated at a gold price of A$4,000/oz, effective 30 June 2026; Mineral Resource 4.23Mt at 3.16g/t for 429koz [1][9]. The company gives a mine life of about six years at its 30,000oz a year target [2][9]. At the rate of the last two quarters (5,563oz and 5,692oz, about 22,500oz a year) the reserve lasts about 8.7 years. The reserve price of A$4,000/oz is below recent AISC, so at that price the mine would not cover its sustaining costs. Maldon has no reserve: Union Hill is an Inferred Resource of 1.31Mt at 4.44g/t for 187koz (2022), plus an 8.6koz low-grade stockpile [1].

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: can Henty lift from about 22,500oz a year (its last two quarters) to its 30,000oz target at close to its FY26 cost of about A$4,250/oz? At the September-quarter gold price that is the difference between a margin of about A$55m a year and about A$24m, and most of the value sits in that difference.
- If it goes our way: Henty makes 30,000oz a year. Its non-royalty AISC in the September quarter was A$25.2m (A$28.4m less A$3.2m of royalties); allow 8% more for the extra tonnes, A$108.8m a year, or A$3,627/oz, plus royalties at 10% of A$6,061, giving AISC of A$4,234/oz, about the FY26 level. Margin (6,061 - 4,234) x 30,000 = A$54.8m a year; less A$10m of growth spending, less 30% tax, leaves A$31.4m a year for 6.6 years (198koz / 30koz). At 10% that is worth A$146.4m. Add net cash after the gold loan, A$31.0m (cash and bullion A$40.0m less equipment finance A$7.5m, insurance funding A$0.9m and the last gold loan payment A$0.6m), less the 2,400oz still owed to Catalyst at A$6,061 (A$14.5m): A$162.9m. Maldon is counted at nil. With all 62.2m options exercised for A$14.1m: A$177.0m on 667.6m shares, A$0.265 a share.
- If it goes against us: Henty stays at the September-quarter rate, 22,768oz a year at A$4,991/oz. Margin A$24.4m a year, less growth spending at the September-quarter rate (A$13.2m), less tax, leaves A$7.8m a year for 8.7 years: worth A$44.0m at 10%. Add A$16.5m of net cash after Catalyst, and take off A$10m for another year of Maldon spending before it is cut back: A$50.4m on 605.3m shares, A$0.083 a share.
- A$500/oz on the gold price moves the margin at 30,000oz by A$15m a year, so the gold price matters as much as the operating question.
- Price: A$0.15 (last trade in the ASX company data fetched 7 Nov 2026; Norgate close 30 Oct 2026 A$0.155), market value A$90.8m. Gap if it goes our way: 0.265 / 0.15 - 1 = +77%. Gap if it goes against us: 0.083 / 0.15 - 1 = -44%.

## 7. Grade
A, deep dive now. The gap if it goes our way is +77%. The question can be answered from filings: Kaiser publishes ounces, costs and AISC by mine every quarter, and the December quarter update (about 8 Jan 2027) and the March quarter (about 10 Apr 2027) both fall inside six months and will show whether Henty reaches 7,500oz a quarter. It is not a value pick: the value if it goes against us is 44% below the price. The deep dive should also test the gold price assumption, since the September quarter's A$6,061/oz is the base for both cases.
