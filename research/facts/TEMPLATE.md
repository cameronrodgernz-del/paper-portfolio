Written <YYYY-MM-DD> by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: <points> points, #<rank> in reports/research_rank.md (<date>).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet?
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification?
- Too thin to trade: median value traded over the last 20 sessions below A$10,000 (ASX) or NZ$12,500 (NZX), so the
  position can't stay within 10% of daily volume over two sessions (rulebook section 3)?
- On Cam's excluded list (config/judgement_excluded.csv)? The gaming, wagering and racing hold ended on 8 Nov 2026,
  when Cam confirmed the list.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? Then it goes to the special
  situations watch list instead (note it in the report back), not a research pick.

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: the one thing a deep dive would settle that the price seems to get wrong or ignore. One or two sentences.
- If it goes our way: what the business would be worth, worked from section 2 (for example: property at its stated
  value less debt; profit back to its five-year average at a named peer multiple; net cash plus the core business at a
  stated multiple). Show the arithmetic.
- If it goes against us: the same, on the bad answer.
- Value it the way the market prices that kind of business, and name the mechanism that gets the price there. Listed
  property: cash earnings after maintenance capital spending, leasing incentives and management fees (AFFO), at the
  peers' AFFO yields, not net tangible assets; the deep dives on Argosy, Investore and Stride found each cheap on NTA
  but priced like its peers on AFFO. Cash or assets that a controlling holder or the board keeps out of holders' reach
  (Step One) count only through a buyback, capital return or bid. NTA or a break-up value is the good case, not "our
  way", unless a sale, return or bid is already under way.
- Only now compare with the price: gap if it goes our way, (value / price) - 1; gap if it goes against us.

## 7. Grade
- A, deep dive now: the gap if it goes our way is 40% or more, the question can be answered from filings, and an event
  inside six months should show the answer. Or, for a value pick (rulebook section 5a), the gap if it goes our way is 40%
  or more and the value if it goes against us is no more than 10% below the price, with no need for a forcing event.
- B, deep dive when its event is close: the gap is 25% or more but the deciding event is further away, or the answer
  needs the event itself.
- C, no deep dive: the gap if it goes our way is under 25%, or a kill fact applies. Say which.
