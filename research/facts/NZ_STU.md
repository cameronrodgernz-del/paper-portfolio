## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. A New Zealand steel distributor and processor (reinforcing, roofing, fastenings, stainless, piping, galvanizing through Perry Metal Protection, bought May 2025). FY26 (year to 30 June 2026) revenue NZ$438.9m, up 13.9% on 115,000 tonnes [1].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? Yes, going-concern doubt. KPMG's opinion on the FY26 accounts is unmodified but includes a "Material uncertainty related to going concern": covenant compliance and liquidity are "highly sensitive" to forecast volumes and margins, and there is material uncertainty over keeping the banking relationship and the fallback options (other borrowing, asset sales, more equity) [2]. The directors' going-concern note says the FY27 forecast assumes a market recovery in the second half, covenants were eased during the year, and the NZ$20m trade loan facility is repayable on demand [2]. FY26 statutory loss NZ$61.2m after a NZ$51.9m impairment; net debt NZ$48.0m; ANZ facilities extended to September 2027 [1][2]. The sheet ends here.
- Too thin to trade? Yes as well. NZX's own daily trading data: median on-market value over the 20 sessions 9 October to 6 November 2026 was NZ$10,523 a day (lowest NZ$1,306, highest NZ$51,526; 6 of the 20 sessions above NZ$12,500) [5]. Over 60 sessions the median is NZ$15,178, so trading has thinned recently. Under rulebook section 3 the largest position would be two sessions at 10% of the 20-session median, about NZ$2,105, below the NZ$2,500 smallest NZX order.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? Yes: a portfolio review and an asset sale process. The review, begun in the fourth quarter of FY26, decided to exit the Reinforcing & Wire and Plate Processing operations (subject to staff consultation). Euro Corporation has offered for the Reinforcing & Wire assets and terms are agreed, conditional on approvals and "satisfactory engagement with the Commerce Commission"; the company is also running a marketing process for the assets "over the next few months", expecting NZ$11m to NZ$12m of asset and inventory value before costs, to pay down debt. Seven smaller sites are to close within 12 months, and the exit of two larger sites is being explored [1]. This belongs on the special situations watch list, noted in the report back. No holder is near 50%: New Zealand Steel 14.31%, Perry Group 8.43%, Lennon Holdings 5.22% at 30 June 2026, and no substantial holder notices were received in FY26 [2].

## 7. Grade
C, no deep dive: kill facts apply (going-concern doubt and too thin to trade, section 1). The portfolio review and asset sale make it a special situation, but the same liquidity limit rules out a trade there too. Next dated events, for the watch list: annual meeting in Auckland on 23 November 2026 (announced) [3]; the Euro Corporation sale and the marketing process "over the next few months" (no date); ANZ facilities to September 2027 [1].
