# Euroz Hartleys Group (ASX:EZL): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 4 points, #57 of 195 ASX names in reports/research_rank.csv (2026-11-08).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. After the sale below it is a Perth private wealth
  business (Euroz Hartleys and Entrust brands, about A$5bn of funds under management, about 60 advisers) [1].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No [5].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$280,387 a day
  (data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz) [12].
- On Cam's excluded list? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no companies; Cam confirmed on 8 Nov 2026 there is nothing to
  avoid at the outset.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? Yes: a business sale whose proceeds are
  still to be paid out. Euroz Hartleys sold its Capital Markets business (stockbroking, research and corporate finance) to
  BMO Financial Group for A$145m cash, subject to completion adjustments; holders approved it on 16 Oct 2026 with 99.99% of
  votes cast, and it completed on 2 Nov 2026 [1][2][3]. The company will pay out the after-tax proceeds as a fully franked
  special dividend of about A$0.498 a share (with about A$0.214 of franking credits) plus an equal capital return of about
  A$0.116, about A$0.614 a share in all, with the final amounts and dates "shortly following completion of all relevant
  month-end procedures"; the capital return also needs an ATO class ruling [1][4]. This goes to the special situations watch
  list.

The pending distribution ends the sheet and sends the company to the special situations watch list. Sections 2 to 6 were not
written, as the template requires once a kill check answer is yes.

For the watch list (facts only, not graded):
- The payout: net proceeds after tax and costs A$101.3m in the pro forma accounts, A$0.614 a share on 164,822,600 shares
  [4]. The Independent Expert (Lonergan Edwards) valued Capital Markets at A$127m to A$143m on a controlling basis and found
  the sale fair and reasonable [4][6].
- What is left: the Private Wealth business. Pro forma (unaudited) profit before tax of minus A$0.55m (FY24), A$1.75m (FY25)
  and A$8.00m (FY26); FY26 profit after tax A$5.4m, of which A$2.47m before tax was a gain on investments and A$2.14m was
  interest income. Pro forma FY26 EBITDA A$7.5m [4]. Pro forma net assets after the payout A$74.6m, including A$21.4m of
  intangibles after an expected A$11.3m brand write-down and A$4.4m goodwill write-off, and a A$20m collateral loan from BMO
  that funds clearing for the BMO business [4]. Net tangible assets after the payout about 32c a share.
- New arrangements: an alliance agreement with BMO (four years, renewable), and Euroz Hartleys keeps trading, clearing and
  settlement for the sold business for four years, with BMO paying the costs [1][3].
- Board and management: Executive Chairman Andrew McKenzie and Managing Director Tim Bunney are moving to BMO and stay
  as non-executive directors "in the short to medium term"; Jay Hughes (co-founder, about 7.29%) is Chair; Alan Logan (formerly
  Shadforth, Godfrey Pembroke and MLC) became CEO on 26 Oct 2026 on A$400,000 base salary; Andrew Fry and Michael Bartley joined
  as executive directors [2][7].
- Holders: substantial holders at 17 Sep 2026 were Andrew McKenzie 8.06%, Ice Cold Investments (Geoffrey Brown family) 7.39%
  and Jay Hughes 7.29%; directors held about 10.65% in June 2026 [3][4][6]. No director bought or sold on market in the last 12
  months (only appointment and departure notices on 2 Nov 2026) [8].
- FY26 group result (before the sale): revenue A$141.7m, profit to members A$16.7m, dividends 7.5c fully franked; NTA per share
  38.2c [5]. EZL joined the All Ordinaries on 21 Sep 2026 [9].
- Dated events: announcement of the final special dividend and capital return amounts and timetable (expected in November
  2026, after month-end procedures [1]); ATO class ruling on the capital return (date not given); AGM 26 Nov 2026, electing
  Hughes, Fry and Bartley [10]; 1H27 result about late February 2027, the first as a wealth business alone (inferred from
  24 Feb 2026 [11]).
- Price for reference: A$1.33, the last trade on 6 Nov 2026 (ASX company data), still with the A$0.614 payout attached. Less
  the payout, the market values the Private Wealth business at about A$0.716 a share, A$118m: about 22 times its pro forma
  FY26 profit after tax, or 32 times without the investment gain [12].

## 7. Grade
C, on a named kill fact: the completed sale of the Capital Markets business with about A$0.614 a share still to be paid out.
Sent to the special situations watch list. Once the payout has gone ex and the first half-year as a wealth business alone is
reported (about February 2027), the remaining business could be looked at again as an ordinary research pick.
