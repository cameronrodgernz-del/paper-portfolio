# Delegat Group (NZX:DGL): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-02 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 4 points, #5 in the NZX table of reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-01).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a

This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. A wine company (Oyster Bay and others) with vineyards and wineries in Marlborough, Hawke's Bay and the Barossa Valley; 3.3 million cases sold in FY26 [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. The auditor's opinion on the FY26 accounts, released with the result on 28 August 2026, is unmodified [1], and the NZX page shows no suspension [6].
- Too thin to trade? Not measured. The NZX page shows NZ$65,687 traded on 30 October 2026 [6], and Investing.com shows a three-month average of 10,610 shares a day (about NZ$44,000 at $4.16) as at early October, a secondary source [7]. Both point to a pass, but neither is a 20-session median.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? Yes. On 23 September 2026 the trustees of the Delegat's Shares Protection Trust (Jakov (Jim) Delegat, chair, Rosamari Delegat, director, and BPM Trustees), who hold 66,857,142 shares or 66.11%, agreed to transfer the whole holding to a new company, Delegat Holdings Limited, in exchange for its shares. The stated value is market value, estimated at about NZ$277m (about $4.14 a share). The notices call it an internal restructuring of the trust; the ultimate holders don't change [3][4][5].

That is a holder over 50% making a move, so the sheet ends here and the company goes to the special situations watch list. On the filings it looks like a holding structure change rather than a step towards a bid, a sell-down or a delisting, but a holding company could later be used for any of those, which is the reason to watch it rather than research it as a pick.

For the watch list: the controlling holding now sits in Delegat Holdings Limited (notice of 23 September 2026). Watch for any further notice from Delegat Holdings, a creep (a holder over 50% may buy up to 5% a year under the Takeovers Code), or any proposal to minority holders. Next dated events: the annual meeting (date not read; director nominations closed after 28 September 2026 [8]) and the 1H27 result, about late February 2027 (inferred from 27 February 2026).

## 7. Grade
C, on a named kill fact: the 66.11% holder's restructuring of 23 September 2026 sends the company to the special situations watch list. Sections 2 to 6 were not written, as the template requires once a kill check answer is yes. If the desk decides an internal restructuring is not a "move" in the sense the check means, the sheet should be reopened; from the FY26 result, operating profit after tax was $61.5m (60.8c a share), statutory profit $39.5m, NTA $5.97 a share and the dividend 22c [1].
