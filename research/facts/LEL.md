# LE Minerals (ASX:LEL): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 5 points, #31 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? Yes: no business to value yet. LE Minerals (formerly Lithium Energy) sold its only advanced asset, the Solaroz lithium project in Argentina, and now holds cash and exploration ground: the Capricorn gold-copper belt in Queensland (51%, rising to 100% if the second tranche completes by April 2027), the White Plains lithium brine project in Utah (100%), and the Burke, Mt Dromedary and Corella graphite projects in Queensland, which it has agreed to sell [1][2]. None has a study or production. The graphite projects carry JORC Mineral Resources but no scoping or feasibility study, and they are being sold to M Battery Materials (MBM) for A$20m, of which A$2m to A$5m in cash and the rest in MBM shares [2][4]. The company also says it is looking for "New Investment Opportunities" in any mineral and at any stage, including "direct equity investments in listed securities" [1]. The sheet ends here.
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No, now. The shares were suspended from quotation for more than a year (they appear on the ASX's long-term suspended lists from April 2025 to January 2026) and were reinstated on 16 March 2026, with the board stating it was funded for at least 12 months [5][6]. In.Corp Audit & Assurance's opinion on the FY26 accounts (year to 30 June 2026) is unmodified [2].
- Too thin to trade? No, narrowly. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$11,331 a day, counting the two sessions with no trades (20 and 21 October) as zero (A$15,772 over the last 20 days that traded), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz [9]. Eight of the 20 sessions were below A$10,000.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? An asset sale is under way (the graphite projects to MBM, subject to a shareholder vote on 20 November 2026 and MBM's float), but no sale of the company and no holder over 50%. Strike Resources (ASX:SRK) holds 31,010,000 shares, 27.69%; LE Minerals was spun out of Strike, and its chairman William Johnson and director Farooq Khan are also executive directors of Strike [2][3].

The sheet ends here on the "no business to value" kill.

For the record, the facts that matter for the special situations watch list:
- Net cash A$56.31m at 30 September 2026 (cash A$8.66m, term deposits A$47.71m, net payables A$0.05m), which the company puts at A$0.503 a share on 112,001,569 shares [1]. It was A$68.27m at 31 December 2025 and A$60.18m (cash and term deposits) at 30 June 2026 [2][6]. The September quarter used A$3.21m on staff, corporate costs and exploration, plus A$1.01m on investments [7].
- What the board says about the cash (30 October 2026): it is applying existing cash to its projects and "retaining the balance of its cash reserves to be applied towards New Investment Opportunities"; the only return of capital planned is the in-specie distribution of MBM shares, and it "may consider future capital management initiatives" [1]. Under ASX escrow rules, 75% of the MBM consideration shares would be distributed only after a 24-month escrow from MBM's listing, and MBM may end the deal if escrow applies and holders don't approve the distribution [4].
- The graphite sale: conditions to be met by 22 December 2026 (extended from 2 November) [4]. MBM's prospectus seeks A$15m to A$25m at A$0.50 a share [8]. General meeting to approve the sale on 20 November 2026; AGM on 27 November 2026 [3][10].
- Directors hold 18,000,000 options at A$0.60 expiring 28 May 2030, issued in June 2026 after holder approval [2].
- FY26 profit after tax of A$48.2m came from the A$58.1m after-tax gain on the Solaroz sale; continuing operations lost A$9.2m (FY25: A$3.8m) [2][11]. The company says its 2024 tax return was lodged on the basis that no tax is payable on the Solaroz sale [1].

## 7. Grade
C, no deep dive: a kill fact applies (no business to value yet, section 1). For the special situations watch list: the company holds about A$0.50 a share of net cash, its board intends to keep most of it for new investments, and its largest holder is a related company. The dated events are the general meeting on 20 November 2026, the AGM on 27 November 2026, the 22 December 2026 deadline for the graphite sale conditions, and the Capricorn second tranche by April 2027.
