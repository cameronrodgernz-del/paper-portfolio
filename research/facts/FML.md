# Focus Minerals (ASX:FML): fact sheet

Written 2026-11-08 by the research desk, from primary sources. Ranking: 5 points, #25 in reports/research_rank.md (2026-11-07).
Stage 3 of the research funnel (rulebook section 5a). A fact sheet replaces the old quick look: it records the facts in a
standard form and asks one question. It does not estimate an expected gain, and it doesn't drop a company on judgement.
This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. A producing gold miner: the Coolgardie Gold Project in Western Australia, with the 1.2Mtpa Three Mile Hill mill, the Bonnie Vale underground mine and the CNX, Dreadnought and Alicia open pits. It sold 49,154 ounces of its own gold in 2025 and 61,008 ounces in the nine months to 30 September 2026 [1][3][5][6]. Ore Reserves of 381,500 ounces (31 December 2025) and a 2022 life-of-mine study give a business to value [1][4].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. RSM's opinion on the 2025 accounts is unmodified; the key audit matters include the tax treatment of the Laverton sale [1]. RSM's review of the half to 30 June 2026 raises no matters [2]. The shares were suspended for one day on 1 April 2025 for lodging the 2024 annual report late, and reinstated on 2 April 2025; the 2024 half-year accounts were restated (mining costs wrongly expensed, A$23.1m) [12].
- Too thin to trade? No. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$234,625 a day (lowest A$112,907), from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz [15].
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? A holder is over 50%, but it has not moved. Shandong Gold International Mining Corporation holds 180,079,908 shares, 62.84% (181,079,908 on its substantial holder notice), and the top 20 holders own 84.30% [1]. There have been no substantial holder notices in 2025 or 2026, no approach and no review [16]. Shandong's people run the board: the executive chairman, Wanghong Yang, is a former Shandong Gold International executive, and the director appointed on 30 March 2026, Wenli Cui, heads the finance department of a Shandong Gold Group subsidiary [1][13]. Noted for the report back; not a kill.

Whether the gap between statutory and underlying profit recurs: the A$203.5m Laverton gain is a true one-off and makes the 2025 earnings multiple meaningless. Coolgardie's own profit is the figure to use. The tenement write-down (A$17.5m) is a one-off in 2025; nothing comparable appears in 1H26 [1][2].

What the headline numbers hide:
- The gold price. All production is sold unhedged to the Perth Mint [2]. Realised prices: A$5,556/oz (2025), A$6,334 (December quarter 2025), A$7,043 (March quarter 2026), A$6,378 (June quarter), A$5,989 (September quarter) [1][3][5][6][14]. The value rests on gold near A$6,000 an ounce.
- Reserves and mine life. Ore Reserves at 31 Dec 2025 were 381,500 ounces: Bonnie Vale underground 165,700 oz at 5.93 g/t, Brilliant open pit 164,000 oz (undeveloped, "undergoing approval process"), CNX open pit 45,200 oz and stockpiles 6,600 oz. They were calculated at A$2,200/oz, from the October 2022 life-of-mine plan [1][4]. Ore mined from Bonnie Vale (44,479 oz) and CNX (8,543 oz) in the nine months to September 2026 leaves about 157,900 ounces in the two producing reserves, roughly six quarters of Bonnie Vale at the September quarter rate, plus Brilliant [3][5][6]. Alicia and Dreadnought are mined outside the reserves. Mineral Resources are 2.59 Moz; the company says less than half of Measured and Indicated has been considered for conversion [1][4].
- Costs per ounce are not published. From the cash flow reports (production, staff, corporate, development, plant and exploration payments, over ounces recovered): about A$3,108 an ounce in the September quarter (387,029 t at 2.22 g/t, 96.6% recovery), A$4,471 in 1H26 (1.85 g/t, with a pre-strip at Dreadnought and toll ore), A$3,895 over nine months [2][3]. Desk arithmetic, before toll-milling income.
- A tax claim. The ATO opened a pre-lodgement review of the Laverton sale, where A$253.5m of past losses were used. If those losses are not allowed, the company estimates about A$88m more tax, before interest and penalties; it judges the treatment probable to stand and has not provided for it [1]. The 1H26 report gives no update [2].
- The controlling holder. Shandong's loans funded the company until the Laverton sale repaid them in 2025, and it now controls a cash pile of about 98c a share. Minority holders get that cash only if the board pays it out [1][7].

## 3. Guidance and record
Focus gives no production, cost or earnings guidance; the AGM presentation states that its contents are "not intended to be forecasts" [4]. Only the AGM slides compare output with the mine plan (March quarter 2026: open pit ore 281.8kt against 263kt planned, Bonnie Vale 86kt against 79.8kt) [4]. There is no record to test.

## 6. The question and the gap
- The question: how long does Coolgardie last at A$6,000 gold, and will the board pay the cash out? The reserves were set at A$2,200 an ounce and cover only about two years of the producing mines plus the undeveloped Brilliant pit; the cash (about 98c a share before tax owed) sits under a 63% holder that has paid one 8c dividend. A deep dive would test the cost per ounce, the chance of re-pricing the 2.59 Moz resource, and the ATO claim.
- How the market prices this kind of business, and the mechanism: a gold producer is valued on the after-tax cash its reserves will produce at the gold price, plus cash that holders can get. Under a controlling holder the cash counts only through a dividend, capital return or bid; the mechanism here would be dividends from 2026 profits and the reserve statement in March 2027.
- If it goes our way: gold stays at the September quarter's A$5,989/oz and all the reserves, Brilliant included, are mined at that quarter's cost of A$3,108/oz. Reserves left about 321,900 oz (121,200 Bonnie Vale, 36,700 CNX, 164,000 Brilliant) at 95% recovery = 305,800 oz. After-tax margin (5,989 - 3,108) x 0.7 = A$2,017/oz, A$616.6m; over about three years, discounted at 10% to the midpoint (x 0.867), A$534.5m. Cash A$279.7m less tax still owed on profits to June (A$28.1m) and an estimated A$18.3m on the September quarter (30% of about A$61.0m of pre-tax profit: 23,434 oz newly sold at A$5,989, less A$66.4m of production, staff and corporate payments and about A$14.4m of depreciation, plus A$1.1m interest) = A$233.3m, all paid out, less A$24.4m of rehabilitation and other long-term provisions. Total A$743.4m on 286,558,645 shares = A$2.59 a share. Each A$500/oz on the gold price moves this by about 32c. Resources beyond the reserves are not counted.
- If it goes against us: Brilliant is not developed, so only the Bonnie Vale and CNX reserves are mined (157,900 oz, 150,000 oz recovered), at the nine-month cost of A$3,895/oz and gold at 2025's average A$5,556/oz: A$174.4m after tax, A$162.4m discounted over about 18 months. The ATO's A$88m is paid. Cash after tax owed A$233.3m, less A$88m and A$24.4m of provisions, plus the mines, gives A$283.2m in all, or A$0.99 a share. This counts all the cash; if the board keeps it, as the template's rule for a controlled company requires, the value is lower still.
- Only now the price: A$1.82 (Norgate close 30 Oct 2026; the ASX's last trade on 6 Nov was A$1.79, ASX company data). Gap if it goes our way: 2.59 / 1.82 - 1 = +43% (+45% at A$1.79). Gap if it goes against us: 0.99 / 1.82 - 1 = -46% (-45%).
- Gold today: the Perth Mint's published Australian dollar spot on 7 Nov 2026 was A$5,891 bid (a secondary source, the Perth Mint's price page [18]). At A$5,900 the "our way" value is about A$2.54: +39% at A$1.82, +42% at A$1.79.

## 7. Grade
A, deep dive now, but only just. The gap if it goes our way is +43% (+45% at the last trade) at the September quarter's realised gold price, and +39% to +42% at today's spot; it rests on gold staying near A$6,000, the September quarter's low cost per ounce and the cash being paid out; at A$5,500 gold it falls to about +25%. The question can be worked from filings (quarterly cash flows, reserve tables, the ATO note), and the 2026 reserve statement and annual report (about 30 March 2027) and the December quarter report (about 30 January 2027) fall inside six months. Not a value pick: the value if it goes against us is 46% below the price. The controlling holder and the undated ATO review are the risks a deep dive must price.
