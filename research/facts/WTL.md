## 1. Kill check
Any "yes" ends the sheet here, with the fact and its link. Nothing else ends it.
- A fund, trust or investment company, or no business to value yet? No. WT Financial runs a national licensee and services platform for financial advice practices (Wealth Today, Sentry Advice, Synchron Advice and Millennium3); it takes a share of the advice revenue its practices bill. FY26 (year to 30 June 2026) gross operating revenue A$245.0m, net operating revenue A$31.7m [1][2].
- Delisting, suspension, going-concern doubt or an auditor's qualification? No. In.Corp Audit & Assurance's opinion on the FY26 accounts is unmodified, and no suspension or going-concern doubt appears in the FY26 results or annual report [1][2][3].
- Too thin to trade? Yes. Median value traded over the 20 sessions 5 to 30 October 2026 was A$6,861 a day, counting the four sessions with no trades (15, 16, 20 and 26 October) as zero, from data/norgate/universe/prices_2026.csv.gz [8]. Over the last 20 days that traded (25 September to 30 October) it was A$17,701; daily values ranged from nil to A$97,901, and 8 of the 20 sessions were above A$10,000. Under rulebook section 3 the largest position would be two sessions at 10% of the 20-session median, about A$1,372, below the A$1,500 smallest ASX order. The sheet ends here.
- On Cam's excluded list, or in the gaming, wagering and racing hold? No. config/judgement_excluded.csv has no entries.
- A takeover, scheme, strategic review, sale process or a holder over 50% making moves? No. The largest holder is UIL Limited (through J P Morgan Nominees) with 23.27%; the founder and managing director Keith Cullen's entities hold 10.57% [3][5]. No approach or review has been announced.

- On 30 June 2026 WT sold its directly operated Vesta Partners advice and accounting businesses to Titan Advice Group, the first "Hubco" of its 50/50 investment joint venture with Merchant Wealth Partners, keeping an equity stake and licensing relationships; it bought a stake in TNT Advisor Solutions on 1 Sep 2026 [1][6][7].
