# 29Metals Limited (ASX:29M): stopped at the first check

Written 2026-11-10 by Claude for the one-company-at-a-time process (rulebook section 5a, docs/RESEARCH_PROCESS.md step 2a). Price at writing: A$0.255 (last sale, ASX company data read 10 November 2026), 1,750m shares, a market value of about A$446m [S7].
Status: rejected at step 2a. The bad case, roughed out from the filings, is clearly more than 30% below the price, so the work stopped there. This is a simulation and not financial advice.

## Why it was looked at
Chosen on 10 November 2026 as the clearest outside buyer on the holder screen after the linked-holder check:
- BUMA, an Indonesian mining contractor (part of BUMA International, formerly Delta Dunia, listed in Jakarta), bought 19.5% in December 2024 [S6];
- BUMA took a board seat in June 2025 and a second in June 2026 [S1];
- BUMA reached 22.13% in March 2026 by taking shortfall from the January raising [S2, S6];
- meanwhile the private equity founder, EMR Capital, has sold down from 47.7% to 18.0%, and AustralianSuper from 17.1% to 11.8% [S6].

## Why it stopped
- The business is losing money at the margin. Golden Grove's all-in sustaining cost was US$6.90 a pound of copper in the September quarter (US$6.05 in June) [S3]. The realised copper price in the first half was US$6.37 [S4], a record level.
- Cash is going out fast. Liquidity fell from A$202m to A$142m in the September quarter. Cash is A$128m against US$110m of drawn debt, so net debt is A$31m [S3].
- The record is one of raisings at falling prices:
  - A$180m in December 2024;
  - A$150m in January 2026 at A$0.40, a 35.5% discount [S2].

  The shares are now A$0.255, 36% below that offer price.
- The plan rests on two things that haven't happened yet:
  - Xantho Extended ramping back up after the 2025 seismic events (mining restarted in October 2026);
  - a restart of Capricorn Copper, which needs a tailings approval and outside funding. The company has had streaming, royalty and joint venture proposals [S3].
- BUMA's last increase was in March 2026, through the raising rather than on market [S6]. Nothing since shows it building toward a bid.

A rough bad case: copper falls back from its record, or Xantho disappoints again, and a third raising at a deep discount takes the shares to about A$0.15. That is about 40% below the price, and it isn't a remote outcome on this record. That fails the 30% limit, and as a special situation the "nothing happens" value isn't stable either, because the business is spending cash.

## What would reopen it
- A bid or proposal from BUMA or anyone else.
- A funding deal for Capricorn Copper that doesn't issue shares, plus two quarters of Golden Grove costs below about US$4.50 a pound.
