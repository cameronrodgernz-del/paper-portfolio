## In short
Gedeon Richter agreed on 17 November 2026 to buy Mayne Pharma by scheme for A$6.40 cash a share, about A$604m, 99.4% above the A$3.21 close on 16 November and 124% above the 30-day average. The board recommends it unanimously, subject to the expert and no better offer, and the three largest holders (about 22%) support it. Beyond the vote, the expert and the Court, it waits on customary regulatory approvals and on the completion of Mayne's sale of its international business to Evaris Pharma, which needs the ACCC. Mayne's A$41.1m of convertible notes due 31 December 2026 and its earlier failed A$7.40 scheme with Cosette (terminated in 2025, then litigated) are the background. MYX traded about A$5.71 to A$5.81 on 17 November, 9% to 11% below the offer.

## Risks to completion
- The Evaris sale and its ACCC review: a condition outside Richter's control; a delay there delays this deal. Rough chance it stops the deal: low, about 5%, until the ACCC timetable is seen.
- Regulatory approvals (FIRB, US antitrust): a Hungarian trade buyer of a US-focused women's health business; Cosette's 2025 bid ran into Treasurer scrutiny, per Inside FMCG. About 5% to 10% until the conditions are read.
- Holder vote: unlikely to fail with a 99% premium and 22% in support.
- Competing bid: possible but no sign of one; the price is below the failed Cosette A$7.40.
- Undisturbed price: no earlier Richter approach was found, so the undisturbed date stays 16 Nov 2026. The Cosette scheme was a different deal that ended in 2025; the share has traded freely since.
