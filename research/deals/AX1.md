## In short
Frasers, which already owned 22.90% of Accent, has been standing in the market since 15 June 2026 offering A$0.65 cash for every share, with sellers paid two trading days after they sell. This isn't a normal takeover: Frasers' own Bidder's Statement says the aim is to reach at least 26%, which under its 2025 subscription agreement lets it ask for a second board seat, and it "does not expect to achieve more than 90%". The bid drew almost no sellers until Frasers declared the price best and final on 28 October, after which it bought about 5.9 million shares in two days and now sits at 23.94%. Compulsory acquisition (90%) is far out of reach, Accent's board says reject, and the shares closed at A$0.655 on 30 October, above the bid, so there's no spread to capture: the bid works as a floor at A$0.65 until 29 January 2027 rather than as a deal that pays out. The event that matters next is the 20 November AGM and possible board spill, where Frasers' extra votes count.

## Conditions
- None. On-market bids must be unconditional (Barrenjoey announcement of 15 Jun 2026: "Given that the Offer is an on-market bid, it is unconditional and the consideration will be paid in cash"). Frasers repeated "the Offer is unconditional and the Offer Price will be paid in cash two Trading Days after acceptance" on 28 Oct 2026.
- Price: fixed at A$0.65 and declared BEST AND FINAL, WILL NOT BE INCREASED (Frasers, 28 Oct 2026). Frasers is held to that statement, so this bid can't be raised.
- Withdrawal: Frasers "reserves the right to withdraw unaccepted Offers in the circumstances permitted by, and in accordance with, Part 6.7 of the Corporations Act" (Barrenjoey, 15 Jun 2026). Nothing suggests it intends to.

## Risks to completion
For a holder the question isn't "will the deal complete" but "will the A$0.65 floor still be there when I want to use it", and "is there any upside above A$0.65".
- Bid withdrawn before 29 Jan 2027: under 5%. Withdrawal is only allowed in the narrow cases Part 6.7 permits, and Frasers is buying at the price it wants. Would rise if something drastic happened at Accent (for example, a large share issue).
- Offer simply lapses on 29 Jan 2027 and the price falls: the main risk for anyone holding above A$0.65. Frasers says the price "may fall after the close of the Offer". Accent shares have closed between A$0.65 and A$0.66 since 7 Oct 2026 (Frasers, 28 Oct), which suggests the bid is holding the price up. Would worsen if Accent's trading stays weak (two FY26 earnings downgrades, per the Bidder's Statement).
- Compulsory acquisition at 90%: under 2%. Frasers needs about 397 million more shares (66% of the company) against a board recommending rejection, and says it doesn't expect to get there.
- Frasers reaching 26%: needs about 12.4 million more shares (2.06%). After the best and final announcement it bought 5.89 million in two days, so this looks reachable before 29 Jan 2027, possibly before the 20 Nov AGM. This doesn't help sellers directly, but it would strengthen Frasers' hand on board seats.
- AGM on 20 Nov 2026: Frasers voted against the 2025 remuneration report (first strike). A second strike would trigger a spill vote. Board turmoil could push the share price either way; it doesn't change the bid terms.

Competing bid prospects (good for holders, kept separate):
- Low, around 5%. Frasers' 23.94% lets it block any scheme (a scheme needs 75% of votes cast) and, after declaring best and final, it can't respond by raising this bid. The IBC has not announced a sale process or any approach. Frasers says no competing proposal has emerged in over four months. A rival would need Frasers to sell to it.
