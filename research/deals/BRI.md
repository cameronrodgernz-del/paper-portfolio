## In short
CTL, a private vehicle run by Shan Kanji that already owns 19.9% of Big River, signed a binding scheme implementation deed on 13 November 2026 to buy the rest for A$1.62 cash a share (about A$156m equity, A$216m enterprise value). That is a 45.9% premium to the A$1.11 close on 12 November. The board backs it unanimously, and Anacacia and NAOS, holding about 59.5% between them, intend to vote for it, so the vote looks safe. The open items are the independent expert, ACCC clearance and the Court; payment is pencilled in for late February 2027. Part of the price may come as a franked special dividend of up to 21c.

## Risks to completion
- Vote fails: about 2%. 59.5% of shares intend to vote for; the board backs it; the premium is large.
- Independent expert finds against: about 3%. A 45.9% premium to the last close makes this unlikely, but the 3-month VWAP (A$1.20) shows the shares traded higher recently.
- ACCC: about 2%. A private building-products and timber group buying a distributor could raise overlap questions depending on Kanji Group's interests; unknown until the ACCC process is seen.
- Material adverse change or contract consents: about 2%. A small cyclical building-products distributor; a weak trading update before the meeting is the thing to watch.
- Delay: the timetable is indicative; a slip of a month or two is common for small schemes.
- Competing proposal: low. The bidder holds 19.9% and the two largest holders have committed intentions, which a rival would have to dislodge.
