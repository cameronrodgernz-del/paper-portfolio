## In short
Genesis Minerals is merging with Vault by scheme: each Vault share becomes 0.7629 new Genesis shares plus about A$0.5125 cash (the A$0.475 headline cash was lifted to make up for Genesis' A$0.05 final dividend). Genesis outbid an earlier Regis Resources deal in July, Regis declined to match, and Vault paid Regis a A$50.7 million break fee. The ACCC waived notification in August, BDO says the scheme is fair and reasonable, the booklet is out, and the vote is on 11 November with payment scheduled for 26 November 2026. What remains is the vote, the Court and no adverse event at either company; the main thing that moves the value holders receive is the Genesis share price, which has fallen about 15% since the booklet was written.

## Risks to completion
- Vote fails: about 3%. Board unanimous, BDO says fair and reasonable, no holder near a 25% block, and the register is dominated by index and active funds. What could raise it: at Friday's prices the default consideration is worth about A$5.43 (A$0.5125 + 0.7629 x A$6.45), down from about A$6.33 when the booklet was written and now below BDO's preferred value for a Vault share of A$5.682 (though still inside its A$4.697–A$6.345 range). BDO valued the consideration on its own numbers, not the market price, so this isn't a fairness trigger, but it gives "vote no" holders a talking point.
- Material adverse change, prescribed occurrence or regulated event at either company: about 1–2%. Both are working gold miners; Vault reported an incident at King of the Hills in April 2026. Would rise with a major operating event or a sharp fall in the gold price.
- Court refusal: under 1%. Nothing contentious disclosed.
- Delay: low. The timetable has about seven weeks of room before the 14 Jan 2027 End Date, and no regulatory approvals are left.
- Market risk on the holding (not a completion risk): the scrip leg moves with Genesis. VAU closed at A$5.40 against an implied A$5.43 on 30 Oct 2026, a gross spread of about 0.6%. To lock that in you would short 0.7629 GMD per VAU share, which the paper portfolio may not be able to simulate.

Competing proposal prospects (good for holders, kept separate):
- Low, around 2–3%. Regis walked away rather than match, the deed has no-shop and no-talk terms with a matching right, and a A$55.5 million reimbursement fee applies. Vault had already done due diligence with Genesis in late 2025, which suggests the field was well tested.
