## In short
I Squared Capital is buying oOh!media by scheme for A$1.68 cash a share; the extra 2c that made up the A$1.70 headline was an interim dividend already paid in September, so it's gone for anyone buying now. Grant Samuel says the deal is fair and reasonable, the ACCC and NZ OIO have cleared it, and holders approved the scheme on 2 November 2026 (99.89% of votes cast, 90.53% of holders voting). On 30 October oOh! said FIRB is the one approval left: FIRB asked for more time and extended its deadline to 13 November, so the second Court hearing moved from 4 to 18 November and payment moved from 26 November to 10 December 2026. If the board pays the planned A$0.10 franked special dividend, holders get A$1.58 plus A$0.10 rather than A$1.68 in one payment.

## Risks to completion
- Vote fails on 2 Nov: about 3%. The board backs it, the expert says fair and reasonable, the price came from a contested sale process, and no holder is near a 25% block. A$1.68 sits just under the midpoint of Grant Samuel's A$1.59–A$1.78 range, which gives a no-vote some argument. A low headcount vote is the other thing to watch.
- FIRB approves late or with conditions: about 10% chance of a further delay past 13 Nov 2026, which would push the 18 Nov Court date and the 10 Dec payment; around 1–2% chance of refusal or unacceptable conditions. A US-based infrastructure investor buying outdoor advertising sites is not an obvious national security case, but FIRB extending its own deadline is a small flag. Would improve on any FIRB announcement before 13 Nov.
- Court refusal: under 1%. The 30 Oct transaction-costs amendment needs Court approval and is described as removing a possible price reduction, so it shouldn't attract objection.
- Material adverse change or contract issues: about 1–2%. No issues disclosed.
- Delay protection: if delays run past 31 Dec 2026, the daily A$0.000136 (about 0.3c a month) partly compensates holders.

Competing proposal prospects (good for holders, kept separate):
- Low, around 2%. The sale was a competitive process (Pacific Equity Partners, I Squared and Oaktree all reconfirmed proposals on 10 Jul 2026, per oOh!'s 13 Jul release); for parties that took part, the 3% higher hurdle for a Superior Proposal only applied for four weeks from 10 Aug 2026 (amended after ASIC talks), so a losing bidder could return with any better offer, but none has. BidCo has matching rights and the meeting is tomorrow.
