## In short
EQT Infrastructure, through Sedum Group, has agreed to buy Cleanaway by scheme for A$3.095 cash per share less any dividends Cleanaway pays before the deal, with a fully franked special dividend of about 14.5 to 19 cents expected inside that price. The deed was signed on 19 October 2026 and nothing has been filed publicly yet: no booklet, no expert report, and no announced FIRB or ACCC lodgement. Payment by the end of March 2027 depends on FIRB, the ACCC (under the new mandatory regime) and two Middle East competition approvals arriving before a Second Court Date in early March 2027. A ticking fee of A$0.0002 per share per day starts on 1 June 2027, which gives holders some protection against slippage. The last price was A$3.00, about 3.1% below A$3.095 before counting franking credits.

## Risks to completion
- FIRB timing: EQT funds are foreign and Cleanaway runs engineered landfills, liquid treatment plants and refineries across Australia, which FIRB may look at closely, so standard conditions (for example on tax) are likely and the review may run several months. Rough chance it delays implementation past end March 2027: 30%; chance it stops the deal: 2%. An announcement that FIRB has been lodged would firm up the timing.
- ACCC: EQT is a financial buyer, so a Phase 1 determination is the likely path; if notified around 10 Nov 2026, the 30 Business Day Phase 1 would end around late December 2026 or January 2027 before any extension. Rough chance of a Phase 2 review: 10%; chance of a block: 2%. Any overlap with EQT's other Australian holdings would raise this; none was identified in the documents reviewed.
- Kuwait and Saudi filings: Cleanaway has Middle East operations, so these are likely to be needed; usually formalities but they add timing risk. Rough chance of delay past March 2027: 10%.
- Specified Contract consents by 14 Dec 2026: for Sedum's benefit and can be waived or the date moved. Rough chance it causes a problem: 5%.
- Shareholder vote and expert: 32.1% premium to the undisturbed A$2.37 price, unanimous board, about 20x FY26 underlying EBIT. Rough chance the vote fails or the expert finds against: 5%.
- Holder protection: the ticking fee from 1 Jun 2027 compensates for delay beyond the end of May; the reverse break fee does not cover regulatory failure.
- Competing bid (helps holders): Cleanaway ran exclusivity with EQT since August, and EQT has matching rights. Another infrastructure fund could still bid during the scheme period. Rough chance of a rival proposal: 5%.
