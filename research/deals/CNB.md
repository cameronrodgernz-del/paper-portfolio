## In short
This deal is not stale; it is in its final days. Evolution Mining is acquiring Carnaby Resources by scheme for 0.0682 new Evolution shares per Carnaby share. Holders approved it on 26 October 2026, the Supreme Court of Western Australia approved it on 29 October and it became Effective on 30 October, when Carnaby shares were suspended. The record date is 3 November and the new Evolution shares are due to be issued on 10 November 2026. Equity value at the offer was about A$213m on a fully diluted basis (company figure at A$0.772 implied), so it is above the A$100m threshold; at Evolution's last price of A$11.76 the consideration is worth about A$0.802 a share.
