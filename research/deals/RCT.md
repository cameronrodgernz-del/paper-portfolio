## In short
This is a mop-up bid. Iris already took control of Reef Casino Trust (the Cairns casino trust) under its first bid, which closed in August 2026 with 85.77%, and on 24 September 2026 made a second, unconditional cash offer of A$4.67 a unit for the rest. The independent expert values the units at A$3.81 to A$4.18 and calls the offer fair and reasonable, and the independent directors recommend accepting. Iris now has 96.50%, enough to force out the remaining holders at the same price, and the offer closes on 9 November 2026 without extension. The equity value at the offer price is about A$232.6 million (49,801,036 units), but only about 3.5% of the units are still outstanding and trading is thin. The last trade was A$4.65 (bid 4.62, ask 4.65), so the remaining gross gain is about 0.4% to A$4.67, paid by late November if accepted.

## Conditions
- None: the offer is unconditional (bidder's statement, 24 September 2026).
- Compulsory acquisition: Iris holds a relevant interest in 96.50%, which entitles it to compulsory acquisition under Part 6A.1. It intends to exercise that right "either before or shortly after the end of the Offer Period" (supplementary bidder's statement, 28 October 2026).

## Risks to completion
- Break risk: very low, under 1%. The offer is unconditional and the bidder already holds 96.50%.
- Timing: units not accepted will be compulsorily acquired at the same A$4.67, but paid perhaps one to two months later.
- Distribution adjustment: any distribution with an entitlement on or after 24 September 2026 that Iris doesn't receive reduces the price paid. None has been announced.
- Liquidity: very thin (volume of 762 units on the last quote), so the small spread may not be tradeable in size.
- Competing bid: nil in practice, since Iris holds 96.50%.
