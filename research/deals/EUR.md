## In short
This is a live deal that is all but finished, not a stale one. Critical Metals Corp. (Nasdaq: CRML) is acquiring European Lithium by schemes of arrangement; holders get 0.045 new CRML shares per EUR share, because CRML's share price fell below the US$8.00 collar floor and the maximum ratio applied. Both schemes were approved on 22 October 2026, the Court approved them on 26 October and they became Effective on 27 October, when EUR shares were suspended. The record date was 29 October and the new CRML shares are due to be issued on 5 November 2026, trading on Nasdaq from 6 November. The deal is well over the A$100m threshold: about A$995m at the announced terms (A$0.58 implied on 1,716,366,113 shares) and about A$810m at the final ratio (0.045 x US$7.25 Scheme VWAP / 0.7021 = about A$0.465 on 1,742,542,187 shares).

## Risks to completion
- Implementation failure: the schemes are binding and CRML has signed a deed poll; the remaining step is the share issue. Rough chance holders are not paid on or close to 5 Nov 2026: 2%. US settlement can slip a day because of time zones, which the company flagged.
- Value risk rather than completion risk: holders receive CRML shares, so what they get depends on CRML's Nasdaq price; the ratio is now locked at 0.045. Sale facility holders take the price achieved within 25 Business Days.
- Competing bid: not possible now that the schemes are Effective.
- Note for the database: the expert found the Share Scheme not fair (but reasonable), which is unusual and worth recording.
