## In short
Tabcorp has agreed to buy BetMakers by scheme for A$0.24 cash a share, with an option to take some Tabcorp shares instead (capped at 25% of the total, and priced off a A$1.00 floor that makes scrip worth less than cash while Tabcorp trades at A$0.89). The deal is at the regulatory stage: the ACCC began a Phase 1 review under the new mandatory regime on 15 October and must decide by 26 November 2026, and gaming and racing consents are needed in several countries. No scheme booklet, expert's report or meeting date exists yet; the companies still target completion in January to March 2027, against an End Date of 10 May 2027. BET closed at A$0.215 on 30 October, an 11.6% gross spread, which shows the market sees real risk in the ACCC review and the overseas licensing consents.

## Conditions
- ACCC: open. Notified under the mandatory regime (MN-20040), Phase 1, effective notification 15 Oct 2026, determination period ends 26 Nov 2026; no notice of competition concerns listed (ACCC register). The deed condition accepts a waiver, an unconditional or acceptable conditional determination, a public benefit determination or a Tribunal decision, but Tabcorp is not obliged to seek a public benefit determination or Tribunal review if the ACCC says no (deed clause 3.4).
- Gaming / racing regulatory consents: open. Each Relevant Authority must consent and confirm BetMakers' Material Authorisations continue on acceptable terms before 8.00am on the Second Court Date. Authorities not named, but the deed refers to US authorities, and Global Tote (about 60% of FY25 revenue, per Tabcorp) runs tote hosting and international pooling and commingling, which is licensed business. BetMakers' 29 Oct 2026 quarterly says the parties "continue to progress" these.
- Independent Expert's Report concluding best interests: open (not yet issued).
- BetMakers holder approval at the Scheme Meeting: open (cannot be waived).
- Constitution amendment removing the Gaming Restrictions, passed as a special resolution at a General Meeting right after the Scheme Meeting: open.
- Court approval: open (cannot be waived).
- No restraining order: no issue disclosed.
- No BetMakers Material Adverse Change or Prescribed Event; no Tabcorp Prescribed Event: none disclosed.
- BetMakers equity incentives dealt with before the Second Court Date (no incentives left on issue at the Record Date): open, procedural. Tranche 2 LTIP vesting was reported met on 29 Oct 2026.
- ASX quotation of new Tabcorp shares: open, procedural.

## Risks to completion
- ACCC moves to Phase 2: about 25%. Tabcorp runs TAB wagering, Sky Racing and tote operations; BetMakers supplies wagering platforms, tote systems and racing data to other operators, so the ACCC may look at whether Tabcorp could disadvantage rival bookmakers who rely on BetMakers. A Phase 2 review would run up to a further 90 business days, pushing a decision to roughly April 2027 or later, close to or past the 10 May 2027 End Date. Would fall if the ACCC clears on or before 26 Nov 2026.
- ACCC blocks the deal: about 5–10%. Tabcorp doesn't have to fight a refusal, and there's no reverse break fee for a regulatory failure. A notice of competition concerns on the register would be the warning sign.
- Gaming and racing consents slow or conditional: about 15–20% chance of a delay beyond Q3 FY27; low chance of outright failure. The authorities aren't named, so progress can't be tracked from public registers. Would rise if the End Date is extended or the timetable slips in the next quarterly.
- End Date squeeze: the deal has about six months from now to become Effective. Any combination of Phase 2 and slow licensing consents would need both parties to agree an extension.
- Vote fails: about 5%. A 45.5% premium to the last close before announcement (A$0.165 on 7 Aug 2026), directors holding about 10% in favour, and event-driven funds (Harvest Lane, Trium) on the register. The extra constitution vote also needs 75%. Would rise if the expert's value range comes in above A$0.24.
- Independent Expert finds against: about 5%. Not yet issued.

Competing proposal prospects (good for holders, kept separate):
- Low, around 5%. The break fee is small (A$2.83 million), which helps a rival, but Tabcorp has a 5-business-day matching right, and BetMakers' main customers and partners are other wagering operators. No rival interest has been disclosed.
