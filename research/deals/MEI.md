## In short
Lynas is buying Meteoric, owner of the Caldeira ionic clay rare earths project in Minas Gerais, Brazil, by scheme for 0.0207 new Lynas shares per Meteoric share. The deed was signed on 1 October 2026 and the deal is at the start: no booklet, no expert report, no court dates. The deciding factor is Brazil's new critical minerals law (Law No. 15,506 of 16 September 2026), which gives a new council, the CIMCE, power to approve ("homologate") changes of control of companies holding critical or strategic mineral rights. The council's rules, timelines and even whether this deal needs approval are not yet settled, and the deed's End Date is only 1 April 2027 (extendable to about 1 July 2027). At last prices (LYC A$12.10, MEI A$0.22) the implied value is about A$0.2505 a share, about 14% above the market price, which shows the market prices in real doubt about timing and completion.

## Conditions
- Brazilian change of control approval (clause 3.1(e)): applies only if, before the Second Court Date, a Brazilian approval "becomes required by law" as a result of Bill No. 2,780/2024, Bill No. 4,443/2025 or other Brazilian critical minerals legislation. The law has now been enacted as Law No. 15,506/2026 (in force on publication, 16 Sep 2026). Article 3 section 2 and Article 41 give the CIMCE, with the national mining agency (ANM), a screening ("triagem") power to homologate changes of control, including indirect ones. Decree No. 13,118/2026 of the same date puts the decision with the CIMCE Executive Committee, which can find the rules do not apply, approve, approve with conditions, or refuse; the Committee meets monthly, needs an opinion from the Ministry of Mines and Energy, and the detailed procedure is left to internal rules (regimento interno) not yet published. Neither the law nor the decree sets a decision deadline. Article 45 of the law requires the CIMCE to be formally installed within 90 days of 16 Sep 2026, so by about 15 Dec 2026. Lynas is responsible for applying and is not required to accept a "materially burdensome condition". Status: no application or ruling announced as at 1 Nov 2026. Lynas' 29 Oct 2026 quarterly describes it as "any approval that may become required".
- Independent expert concluding the scheme is in Meteoric shareholders' best interests: open; report not released.
- Meteoric shareholder approval (cannot be waived): open; meeting expected January 2027.
- Court approval under s411(4)(b) (cannot be waived): open.
- ASIC and ASX consents before 8.00am on the Second Court Date: open.

- No Meteoric prescribed event or material adverse effect, and none for Lynas: no issue announced. Note the Caldeira Installation Licence (LI) from FEAM is now expected early 2027 rather than late 2026 (29 Oct 2026 update); Meteoric says this will not affect the scheme timetable.

## Risks to completion
- Brazil CIMCE approval, timing (main risk): a brand new body with no published procedure or deadline, still being set up, and a high-profile rare earths asset going to a foreign owner. The ANM and the Ministry of Mines and Energy both feed in. Rough chance it pushes implementation past early March 2027: 50%. What would move it: publication of the CIMCE internal rules, an announcement that Lynas has filed, or a ruling that the regime does not apply ("não incidência"). The 3-month End Date extension and the further A$75m of Lynas funding linked to an extension give room for a delay into mid-2027.
- Brazil CIMCE approval, refusal or burdensome conditions: Lynas can walk away from a materially burdensome condition, and a refusal or non-approval by the End Date lets either party terminate (with Lynas paying the A$8.8m reverse fee). Rough chance this stops the deal: 10%. Lynas says it has engaged directly with the Federal Government and BNDES, which helps; a change of political tone after Brazil's October 2026 elections would hurt.
- Shareholder vote: retail-heavy register; only about 9.3% has stated voting intentions, and Kumova can sell. A fixed ratio means a falling Lynas price cuts the value holders get (implied value has fallen from A$0.286 at announcement to about A$0.25). Rough chance the vote fails: 7%.
- Independent expert: not yet appointed publicly or reported. Rough chance of a "not in best interests" finding: 5%.
- Caldeira licensing and material adverse effect: the LI is delayed to early 2027 after FEAM information requests. Rough chance this is used to end or reprice the deal: 5%.
- Competing bid (helps holders): Lynas is a strategic buyer and the asset is rare; other Western rare earths groups could be interested, but the same Brazilian approval would apply to them and Lynas has matching rights. Rough chance of a rival proposal: 5%.
