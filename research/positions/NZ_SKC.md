## The values

- The business alone: NZ$0.748 (Valuation Summary D12), from bear NZ$0.24, base NZ$0.78 and bull NZ$1.30, weighted 25/55/20. The 28 October model had NZ$0.700.
- With a takeover, an Adelaide sale and a forced share issue laid over the three cases: NZ$0.76 (NZ$0.756 to NZ$0.776), about 8% above the price.
- With every cautious view applied: NZ$0.64. If FY28 savings come in NZ$10m short in every case, the value is NZ$0.70 and the gap is gone.
- Required return 11.7% a year. If the shares reach value by August 2028 the return is 16.5% a year.
- What moved it from NZ$0.700: lower bond yields (+3.1 cents), tax losses that had been left out (+2.0), a fix to how the class action is counted (+1.4), and a smaller online casino market, now Evergreen's own estimate of NZ$760m to NZ$1,040m of licensed revenue (-2.1).

## What would change the view

Better: half-year EBITDA of NZ$85m or more, the Grand settled, Adelaide sold near NZ$180m, or a firm bid. Worse: half-year EBITDA under NZ$78m, the Grand consent slipping past December, Allan Gray selling below 10%, or pub pokie spend rising while SkyCity's falls for a second year.
